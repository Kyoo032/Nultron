# Mascot

Nultron is the desk's one character, drawn by one component, `NultronMascot` (`apps/web/components/nultron/nultron-mascot.tsx`): a root `chat-mascot` with `data-mascot="nultron"`. What it does is data, 21 states in `apps/web/lib/mascot-states.ts`; what it looks like is a set of pre-rendered 3D images named by `apps/web/components/nultron/images/manifest.json` (a still per state, a one-shot clip for six states, a four-frame loop for thirteen working states). Job desks place it through `MascotSlot`; Chat's hero and live turn are in [chat.md](./chat.md). Since 2026-10-01 it also stands in the rail as the **desk buddy** (`apps/web/components/desk-buddy.tsx`), the one status character while it is on screen: the sub-features and recipes for it are `desk-buddy*` below. The map is [`docs/internal/maps/mascot-and-brand.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/mascot-and-brand.md).

Product: both · Instance: main :3000

## Sub-features

- `desk-buddy` — `data-testid="desk-buddy-dock"` is the empty end of the rail's list (the last child of `nav[data-tour="rail-modes"]`), with `data-view` `body`, `chip` or `none`. `desk-buddy` (a group, `aria-label` from `common.buddy.region`) holds `desk-buddy-tap` (the button that carries the name and rotates `love`, `lets-go`, `celebrating`) and `desk-buddy-minimise`; its mascot has `data-placement="buddy"`, `data-variant="full"`, a box of 56 to 96 px, `aria-hidden`. `desk-buddy-chip` (in `rail-footer`, beside the theme control) holds a 32 px `data-variant="head"` mascot. `data-view` is also `none` whenever a page stage is on screen (see `buddy-stage`; `buddyView`, `apps/web/lib/desk-buddy-state.ts:106-108`), so on a page with a header stage or a thread the rail shows nothing. The sizes that follow are for a page with no stage and no other full body: at 1920x1080 the body is 96 px, at 1440x900 it is a body whose size depends on the desk, and at 1366x768, 1280x800, 1100x800 and 1024x768 there is only the chip (the rail's spare room is what decides it, nothing else). The size is `min(96, slack - 6, list width - 16)`, shown only from 56 (`bodySize`, `apps/web/lib/desk-buddy-state.ts:95-99`), and the slack is the rail's free height under its last row, so it moves with the rows the desk's rail holds. Do not pin a number: assert 56 to 96. Measured at 1440x900 on `/settings`: 2026-10-01 about 61 px, driven 2026-10-05 on the operator's desk 89 px. A collapsed rail (and so a phone) has none of the three. A desk with every agent hired (13 agent tiles at 1440x900, confirmed live 2026-10-16) leaves no slack in the rail, so the buddy is the chip there (`apps/web/lib/desk-buddy-state.ts:106-110`).
- `desk-buddy-one-character` — the buddy draws nothing (`desk-buddy-dock` `data-view="none"`) while any other full-body `chat-mascot` is visible (the Chat hero, an empty desk's character, onboarding), and steps in 350 ms after the last one leaves. A page stage (`buddy-stage`) stands the rail's buddy down the same way: while one is on screen the dock is `data-view="none"` and the stage's buddy is the one status character. The buddy **takes status** (the hook `useBuddyTakesStatus` is at `apps/web/lib/desk-buddy-store.ts:333`; the `takesStatus` flag it reads is computed at `:115`) while a dock is mounted or a stage is on screen and no other full body is visible; while it does, the chip beside a job, the live turn's head, the chat error line's head, the installer panel's and the updates panel's heads are not drawn at all; their text status is.
- `buddy-stage` (2026-10-01) — a page can hold the buddy itself (`components/buddy-stage.tsx`). `data-testid="buddy-stage-header"` sits in the art column of every `ModeHeader` except Settings, Workspaces and the chat-first `/workspaces` page (they pass `shapes={false}`: `settings-page.tsx:420`, `workspaces-page.tsx:212`, `virtual-agents-page.tsx:164`), from 60rem of header width, in a disc of the mode's colour, resting in the page's own pose (`MASCOT_MODE_HOME`: Documents `writing`, Research `searching`, Market `charting`, Finance `calculating`). Chat has no `ModeHeader`; it uses `buddy-stage-composer` instead, which stands on the composer's top edge in a thread (not on the empty Chat, which has the hero). Each carries `buddy-stage-*-tap`, and its mascot has `data-placement="buddy"`. A stage waves when it mounts (`data-state="wave"` for `WAVE_MS`, 1.4 s, `lib/desk-buddy-state.ts:45`), then holds the page's pose, and sleeps (`data-state="sleep"`) while `<html data-ambient-reason="idle">` and nothing is claimed (`components/buddy-stage.tsx`); a run, a job, a tap or a flash from the store overrides both. It counts as on screen once a quarter of it is visible (`FULL_BODY_VISIBLE_RATIO`). While a stage is on screen `desk-buddy-dock` is `data-view="none"`, the buddy is the status character, and an empty desk's own `data-placement="empty"` character is not drawn unless `?mascot=<state>` is set (`components/mascot-slot.tsx`; `StudioEmptyDesk` gets `data-staged="true"`). Below 60rem the header's art column is `display: none`, so it is not a stage and the rail buddy and the empty-desk character come back.
- `desk-buddy-reactions` — resting `idle`; `sleep` while `<html data-ambient-reason="idle">` (12 s without input) and nothing is claimed; `wave` when it steps into view and on the first visit of a mode (at most one in 20 s); the chat run (`lets-go` 0.9 s, `thinking` busy, `answering` busy, a 1.8 s `celebrating` when the reply lands, `error` for 2.4 s on a failure); a job's phase pose busy (the same table as `mascot-phase`), then `celebrating` or `error` when it ends (not for a cancel); `charging` busy while the first-run installer runs or an update downloads, `surprised` once an update is ready; `surprised` once on a blocked gateway gate; `presenting` while the tour is open. `?buddy=<state>` (with `&buddyBusy=1` for a working pose) is a priority-100 claim, read once when `DeskBuddyReactions` mounts (`desk-buddy-reactions.tsx:62-70`, so load the URL fresh): it beats every other claim, but a tap, a run-end cheer or an arrival wave is a flash and still shows over it for its `FLASH_MS` (2.4 s, the wave 1.4 s; `compute()` is `flash ? flash.state : claim?.state`, `desk-buddy-store.ts:106-107`).
- `mascot-states` — `data-state` is one of the 21 `MASCOT_STATES`: wave, idle, sleep, thinking, writing, answering, searching, calculating, charting, reviewing, listening, painting, filming, editing, presenting, celebrating, error, surprised, love, charging, lets-go. The accessible name and tooltip come from `common.mascot.<state>` (en and id), unless the mascot is `decorative` (`aria-hidden`, no role, name or tooltip).
- `mascot-picture` — inside the root, `.nx-art` holds `img.nx-still` (the still: `full/<state>` at 64 px and up, `head/<state>` under 64 px), and on the body only a `.nx-strip` (a `once` state with a clip) or a `.nx-loop` (a working state with a loop, **mounted only while `data-busy="true"`**: an idle body has no `.nx-loop` in the DOM and never requests its file). Every mounted image `complete` with `naturalWidth` above 0. `data-clip="true"` and `data-loop="true"` on the root say which the state has. The art carries `data-sheet="ready"` once its strip is loaded and decoded and its still is painted; clip and loop animations run only under it. While a state change is settling, the previous state's art is still in the DOM as `.nx-art.nx-held` (its still only), under the new art.
- `mascot-loading` (2026-09-29) — what a page fetches for the character. Nothing eagerly: `/chat` on a cold load requests 3 images (`full/idle`, `full/wave`, `full/celebrating`, 65.4 KB), `/market` 6 (`full/wave`, `clips/wave`, `full/celebrating`, `full/error`, `full/sleep`, `full/charting`, 249,446 bytes; measured 2026-10-01 over a 4 s window, 208 KB and four images on 2026-09-29). The Chat hero's two clips (`clips/wave`, `clips/celebrating`) are requested on the first `pointerenter`, `pointerdown` or focus inside `chat-hero`, before the orb itself is reached; the onboarding hero's `clips/love` on the key step. Stills of the states a mount point can reach are fetched when the browser is idle. No request ever names a strip that no mounted state can play.
- `mascot-motion` — `data-motion` is the performance contract. `still` (idle, sleep): no animation. `once` (wave, celebrating, error, surprised, love, lets-go): one clip when the state is entered, `frames / fps` long (0.83 to 1.17 s), stepped through its frames, the still hidden meanwhile and back at the end. `loop-busy` (the twelve work states and charging): loops only while `data-busy="true"`, stepped with `steps(4)` at the fps the manifest records for that loop (3, 4 or 6). Without `data-busy` nothing under a mascot has an `Infinity` iteration count.
- `mascot-busy` — `data-busy="true"` appears only when the caller says a job is running (`busy`) and `isMascotBusy` agrees: `data-placement="beside"` (a chip beside a job) or `"buddy"` (the desk buddy) and a `loop-busy` state; an `empty` placement is never busy. A busy mascot keeps moving while the desk is idle or blurred and stops when the window is hidden. A body (64 px and up) swaps its still for the loop strip; a head chip has no strip and takes a small transform loop on its still (nod, bob or swell by state): smooth in a browser, `steps(4)` in the packaged shell (`shell-motion`).
- `shell-motion` (2026-10-05) — the desk's loops have two timings, picked by `<html data-shell>` (`apps/web/lib/shell-motion.ts`, written once by `apps/web/src/main.tsx` before the first render: `desktop` only in the packaged Electron shell, `browser` everywhere else, webdev and the hosted app included). In a browser the ambient set (the header and hero shapes' bob `nx-bob` 4 s and turn `nx-turn` 8 s, the dot grid's drift `nx-grid-drift` 12 s a tile) and the busy transform loops (the working dots, a head's nod, bob and swell) are smooth: `animation-timing-function` reads `ease-in-out` (bob, dots, nod, bob, swell) or `linear` (turn, drift), and the computed `translate` / `rotate` changes on every animation frame. With `data-shell="desktop"` they are the stepped desk it shipped with: `steps(8)` and 6.4 s, 12.8 s, 19.2 s for the ambient set (a value every 400 ms: the bob moves 7 px and 8 degrees over its eight steps, so 0.875 px and 1 degree a step; the turn 11.25 degrees; the grid 1 px every 800 ms. Driven 2026-10-05 on :3000 by flipping the attribute, 3 s of animation-frame samples of `translate` / `rotate` on the four visible `.float-shape-body`: about 35 to 47 distinct values a second in a browser, about 2 to 3 a second under `data-shell="desktop"`, with `steps(8)` and 6.4 s / 12.8 s / 12.8 s / 6.4 s. Sample `translate` and `rotate`, not `transform`, which these loops do not animate. Do not assert per-frame maxima such as "0.35 px": at 60 fps the expected deltas are far smaller, so count distinct values a second), `steps(4)` for the busy transform loops. The pre-rendered frame strips (a clip, a four-frame busy loop on a body) step in both shells. The pause (`html[data-ambient="paused"]`), `[data-busy]`-only busy loops and reduced motion are the same in both.
- `mascot-size` — `data-variant="head"` at box sizes under 64 px, `data-variant="full"` from 64 px. Defaults: `beside` 40 px, `empty` 72 px. Onboarding is 96 px. The Chat hero is sized by CSS (`--orb`, its box is in [chat.md](./chat.md), "Hero").
- `mascot-triggers` — the moments that pick a state (`apps/web/lib/mascot-triggers.ts`). The first-run installer panel (`component-setup`): `charging` and busy while it runs, then `celebrating` or `error`. The rail updates panel (`app-updates-panel`): `charging` and busy while the release downloads, `surprised` when it is ready to install, `error` on a failure, nothing while checking or current. The onboarding hero: `wave` on the welcome step, `idle` on the key step, `love` on the examples step. A new chat's first live turn: `lets-go` for 900 ms, then `thinking` / `answering`. The first-run tour's card (`guide-card`, `apps/web/components/guide-card.tsx:101`) mounts a decorative 36 px mascot beside its title: `wave` while the tour is offered, `presenting` while it is guiding.
- `mascot-pose` — the home state of a desk when no phase has arrived: chat `idle`, documents `writing`, research `searching`, finance `calculating`, data `calculating`, market `charting`, legal `reviewing`, meeting `listening`, images `painting`, videos `filming`, music `listening`, edit `editing`, presentations `presenting`, education `presenting`, knowledge `searching`.
- `mascot-placement` — `data-placement="empty"` on an unused desk that shows the character (only below 60rem of header width, where no page stage stands in, or with `?mascot=<state>`; see `buddy-stage`): Meeting, Market (with no tickers inside `market-studio-empty`; with tickers above the board while no job runs), Finance (the chooser at `/finance` with no `task`, and `finance-studio-empty`), Edit (the agent panel), Education (the chooser at `/education`), Legal (the new-matter screen) and Knowledge (Sources). Documents, Research, Data, Images, Videos, Music and Presentations (restored 2026-10-01) show it after the prompt dock through `StudioEmptyDesk` (`<mode>-studio-empty`, `data-tier` `full` | `mascot` | `none`): only while nothing is on the desk (no result, saved item, running job or progress list), and only as much as the window has room for, so it never moves the dock or the template list and never adds a scrollbar; a `none` tier leaves the wrapper empty and the rail buddy shows. `data-placement="beside"` is the default placement, so the Chat hero's mascot (`chat-hero.tsx:94`, a full body that is never busy), the guide card's 36 px one and the live turn, error line, installer and updates panel heads carry it too: scope a read by `data-variant` or an ancestor, never by placement alone. The job chip sits next to `JobProgressList` (`finance-progress`, `research-progress`, `data-progress`, `market-progress`, `meeting-progress`, `legal-progress`, and `presentations-progress`, mounted while `busy === "generate"` or the job runs, `presentations-studio.tsx:703-705`; Presentations has no `MascotSlot`, and its generate button shows a plain working status), beside a busy generate or export control, and beside Knowledge sources while one is indexing.
- `mascot-phase` — the active `job.phase` / `job.step` id picks the state: planning, distilling, debate, risk → `thinking`; searching, reading, indexing, resolving → `searching`; drafting, minuting, saving, translating, synthesis → `writing`; computing, profiling, analyzing, quotes → `calculating`; verifying → `reviewing`; analysts, macro → `charting`; extracting, transcribing → `listening`. An unknown id uses the mode's home state. `failed` → `error`. `done` with no error → `celebrating`. Presentations adds two more ids from the host (`mascot-states.ts:129-130`, emitted at `packages/host/src/open-slide-generate.ts:166-169` and `:270-273`): designing → `presenting`, repairing → `writing`.
- `mascot-idle-sleep` — an empty desk waves (`data-state="wave"`) for about 1.4 s, settles on the home state, then shows `sleep` after 45 s unused. `?mascot=<state>` forces that state on every `MascotSlot` and skips both timers; `?mascotMode=<mode>` overrides `data-mode` and with it the home state. Driven 2026-10-05 on :3000, `/meeting`: at 900 px wide the visible mascot is the `empty` one (`wave` at 0.9 s, `listening` by 3 s) and the stage buddy is hidden; at 1280 the only mascot is the header stage (`data-placement="buddy"`, `wave` then `listening`) and no `empty` one exists unless forced; `?mascot=sleep`, `love` and `thinking` each set the `empty` slot to exactly that state while the stage still waved or listened, so always scope to `[data-placement="empty"]`.
- `mascot-reduced-motion` — with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `.chat-mascot, .chat-mascot *` get `animation: none !important` in `apps/web/app/globals.css` (`:2773-2779`, the `.chat-mascot` selectors at `:2776-2777` and `animation: none` at `:2778`, inside the `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block that starts at `:2761`) and both `animation: none !important` and `transition: none !important` in `components/nultron/nultron.css` (the `@media` at `:313`, the rule at `:314-318`, the busy-still rules at `:320-329`). The picture stays: assert `data-state` and the displayed still, not that something moves. A busy body (`[data-busy][data-loop]`) also gets its still back (`.nx-still` opacity 1, `.nx-loop` 0), because without the animation the loop strip would show its frame 0, a 320 px copy of the still.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open a desk with an empty-desk character on Default: `/meeting`, `/market`, `/finance` (no `task`), `/edit`, `/education` (no `task`), `/legal` (new matter), `/knowledge` (Sources), and the empty landing of `/documents`, `/research`, `/data`, `/images`, `/videos`, `/music` and `/presentations`. The character waves, then settles on that desk's state — but only while no page stage stands in for it: from 60rem of header width the header's `buddy-stage-header` is the character and the empty desk draws none (below 60rem, or with `?mascot=<state>`, it is drawn). `/meeting` draws it once, in the main pane (the recordings list says only "No meetings yet.").
- Start a job on a desk that has a phase list or a busy generate control: a 40 px head appears beside it and follows the phases (only where no buddy takes status; otherwise the buddy works with the job, see below).
- Open `/chat`: the empty desk's hero shows the whole body; hover it to see a wave, tap it for a cheer. Send in a new chat and the buddy (on the composer, in a thread) says "let's go" before it starts thinking; with no buddy on screen the head beside the live answer does.
- First run: the onboarding hero waves, stays calm on the key step, and shows love on the last step. If the document reader has to be fetched, the installer panel on the key step shows the character charging.
- Retired packaged app only (history since 2026-10-14): the updates control in the rail footer shows the character charging while a release downloads, surprised when it is ready.
- Open any page without a full-body character on it (Settings, Usage, Documents once it has a result, any thread in Chat) on a tall window: the buddy stands in the rail's bottom corner, a head chip beside the theme control on a short one. Send a chat message, start a job: it works with you, cheers when the work lands. Hover it for the small control that makes it a head; tap it for a reaction. Leave the mouse alone for 12 s and it nods off.
- Drive hooks, not user controls: `?mascot=<state>`, `?mascotMode=<mode>`, `?mascot=<state>&mascotBusy=1`, `?buddy=<state>` and `?buddyBusy=1`.

## Driving it with the Nultron harness

Preconditions:

- Doctor exits 0 against `http://127.0.0.1:3000`. `:3000` may run with a real key (`runtime: "ai"`): the recipes below start no job and send nothing unless they say a stub desk is required.
- Use your own tab (`tabs_create`), not the shared fronted one, and a custom viewport of 1280 x 800 unless a step says otherwise. Reset it with `preset desktop` at the end. The Electron harness that loads `:3000` at an exact size (a `BrowserWindow` with `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride` and `Emulation.setFocusEmulationEnabled`) is the closer match to the packaged app and is what produced the 2026-09-29 evidence.
- Scope every read to the mascot you mean, by placement. `[...document.querySelectorAll('[data-testid="chat-mascot"]')].find((e) => e.checkVisibility())` is **not** enough at 1280 x 800 on a page with a header stage (`/meeting`, `/market` and every `ModeHeader` page): the first visible `chat-mascot` there is the header stage's buddy (`data-placement="buddy"`, 116px, the page's own pose), not the slot's. Driven 2026-10-01: the 21-state probe read `listening` for all 21 states, and failed all 42 loads, until it was scoped. For the empty-desk character use `find((e) => e.dataset.placement === "empty")`; for a stage `[data-placement="buddy"]`; for a chip beside a job `[data-placement="beside"]`; or `[data-testid="chat-mascot"][data-mode="<mode>"]`.

Recipes:

- **Smooth in browsers, stepped in the packaged shell (2026-10-05).** Headless Chromium through `playwright-core` (`--disable-gpu` mimics the packaged app; `channel: "chromium"` with no flag is a GPU-composited browser), 1280 x 800, `/documents` (any page with a mode header and the dot grid; Chat has its own sky and no grid), a `requestAnimationFrame` sampler for 4 s inside the first 12 s after load (no input, so the desk is still `active`). ## Gotchas

- The buddy is in the rail, so `getByTestId("chat-mascot").first()` can be the buddy, the hero or an inline chip: scope to `[data-testid="desk-buddy"]`, `desk-buddy-chip`, a stage (`buddy-stage-header`, `buddy-stage-composer`), `[data-placement="beside"]` or `[data-placement="empty"]`. Right after a load the buddy is not there for about 350 ms and, on a page with its own character, not at all.
- The buddy's pose is whatever the app last told the store; a drive that opens a page with a job pane kept alive in the background may find it busy for that job. `?buddy=<state>` holds it.
- Hovering the buddy shows `desk-buddy-minimise` only on a pointer that can hover; a touch device always shows it.
- Visited modes stay mounted (SKILL.md **G3**). After Chat, `getByTestId("chat-mascot").first()` and `document.querySelector('[data-testid="chat-mascot"]')` return the hidden hero first; scope to the visible mascot or to `[data-mode="<mode>"]`.
- `/meeting` has one visible empty-desk mascot at every width (it had two until 2026-09-29, one in the recordings list). The main pane's follows `?mascot=`.
- **Bytes and blank frames need a cold context.** A new Playwright context per measurement; count `Image` requests under `components/nultron/images/` (a dev server also serves each file as a `?url` module, which is a `Script` request, not an image). A `<html>` reload keeps the warm-up's `Image` objects, so measure the first load of each page. With CDP `Network.setCacheDisabled` on, a new `<img>` requests its URL again even if it was warmed; that is expected, and the held art is what keeps the box from going empty meanwhile.
- **`data-busy` from the console no longer shows a loop.** The loop is mounted by React only while the mascot is busy. Use `?mascot=<state>&mascotBusy=1` (see the Busy loops recipe).
- The desk remounts its mascot about 300 ms after load, when the workspace id arrives. An element found before that detaches; a `once` clip restarts on the new element. Wait for a stable element and query again after any wait.
- The Chat hero ignores `?mascot=`; only `MascotSlot` reads it.
- A hidden pane freezes transitions and animations, and the hero's drift eases `translate`. A read right after a change can show the start value. Take a screenshot first, or inject `* { transition: none !important }` for the read.
- A hidden Browser pane also produces almost no animation frames (60 to 100 `requestAnimationFrame` calls a second when it happens to be shown, 1 to 4 over 1.5 s when it is not, and `getAnimations()` then stays at the first frame, so a strip's `translateX` reads 0 and a clip looks stuck). Anything timed (a clip's frame steps, a loop's four offsets, reduced motion, dark or 2x rendering) belongs in headless Chromium through `playwright-core` from the repo's `node_modules`, in a script file under the scratchpad, sampling `getComputedStyle(...).transform` inside a `requestAnimationFrame` loop; it renders real frames and never touches the operator's browser storage. For the state and file checks (the 21 states, the manifest files, sizes, no `Infinity` iteration outside `data-busy`) a same-origin `iframe` inside a Browser tab works and is fast, at 1280 x 800 or 375 x 812 (read `checkVisibility()` and `getBoundingClientRect()` inside the frame; finish the entrance animations first with `document.getAnimations().forEach((a) => a.finish())`, or the boxes read 2 to 4 px small).
- The shared fronted Browser tab is driven by other agents as well: a tab you fronted can navigate under you between two calls. Work in your own background tab and pass its `tabId` every time.
- Dark mode is a class on `<html>`, and the app writes `light` during boot. Swap it after the mascot is stable, and put `light` back; the swap stores nothing in `agentforge-theme`.
- `?mascot=` is a drive hook, not a user control. Do not wait 45 s for `sleep` on a normal drive; use `?mascot=sleep`.
- Setting `data-busy` from the console proves the CSS, not the trigger: it bypasses `isMascotBusy`. Prove the trigger on a stub desk with a real job or send.
- A head is 256 px of a close-up, at 24 px `error` and `sleep` look alike. Chips are 40 px.
