# Settings

Settings is where the owner pastes a Toko Token gateway key from api.tokotokenai.com. There is no login. The page shows the key field, Save, a compact this-key usage strip with Open Usage, privacy note, and runtime status. There is no Advanced tab, no Extras, no Build/Agents links, and no injection-guard bypass in the UI. Below the privacy note sits the **Start over** card (on a hosted desk `auth-account`, `account-plan` and `settings-storage` sit between them, `settings-page.tsx:586-591`): sign out of the gateway, or reset the machine to a fresh install. Map: [`docs/internal/maps/settings-and-gateway-gate.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/settings-and-gateway-gate.md).

Product: both · Instance: main :3000

- `settings-reset` (0.14.26) is the **Start over** card after `privacy-note`. The destructive rows sit inside the closed disclosure `settings-reset-danger` (summary `Sign out and delete` / `Keluar dan hapus`). Open it before the rows are visible; a closed `<details>` keeps them in the DOM, so a count assertion passes and a visibility assertion does not. Two destructive rows, no `window.confirm`: `settings-reset-key` opens `settings-reset-key-confirm` (`settings-reset-key-submit`, `settings-reset-cancel`) and POSTs `/api/v1/settings/reset` with `{ scope: "key" }` — the key is forgotten on every desk, threads stay, and the app drops to onboarding. `settings-reset-all` opens `settings-reset-all-confirm` (`settings-reset-all-confirm-name`, `settings-reset-all-submit`, `settings-reset-cancel`); the owner types the literal `RESET` before submit enables, and the POST carries `{ scope: "all", confirm: "RESET" }`. The full-wipe walk is a webdev and desktop walk only; on a hosted desk the proof of the refusal is the 403 and an unchanged desk.
- `settings-storage` (Phase 8) is the **storage card**, rendered only where the host's `storageQuota` capability is true — a hosted tenant, never a desk. One read of `GET /api/v1/storage/usage` feeds it: `settings-storage-used` is the used/limit/percent line with a bar under it, `settings-storage-low` appears at the warning threshold and `settings-storage-full` when the tenant is blocked, and `settings-storage-error` replaces only the used / bar block when the read fails (`settings-storage-card.tsx:163-169`): the "Biggest files" list and its empty line (`:198-222`) and `settings-storage-delete-error` stay, and a read that throws shows both `settings-storage-error` and `-delete-error` (`:113-115`). `settings-storage-items` lists the biggest media objects with `settings-storage-delete-<mediaId>` on each, which sends `DELETE /api/v1/media/:mediaId` and re-reads. `settings-storage-delete-error` (`apps/web/components/settings-storage-card.tsx:225`) shows the card's one `error` state: a delete that was refused or threw (`:132-140`), and also a read that threw (`reload`, `:113-115`). On a desk the card renders nothing **and fetches nothing** — an empty network tab is the proof, not an empty card.
- `settings-reset-tenant` (Phase 8; rebuilt 2026-10-01, SR-65) is the hosted **Erase company account** control, behind the `tenantReset` capability, and it is the other side of the `settings-reset-all` refusal above: it appears only on a hosted build and never on a desk, **and only to a person the host reports as `owner`** (`GET /api/v1/auth/session` carries `role`, the membership row's, which is the portal's word since 2026-10-01: SR-65). An admin, a member, a signed-out browser and a host that sent no role do not see it; the other rows of the card are unchanged. It is a bright red button in `settings-reset-tenant-zone`, **outside** the closed `settings-reset-danger` disclosure on purpose. The first click opens `settings-reset-tenant-confirm` (an `alertdialog`: the title names the act, `settings-reset-tenant-warning` and the paragraph after it are its description) and moves focus into the phrase box `settings-reset-tenant-confirm-name`; the final red button `settings-reset-tenant-submit` stays disabled until the exact phrase `DELETE` is typed (capitals; `RESET` and `delete` do not count). Escape or `settings-reset-cancel` closes it, forgets the phrase and returns focus to the red button; Enter in the box does nothing. The second click POSTs `{ scope: "tenant", confirm: "DELETE" }`. **Erasing deletes every person in the company and ends every session, the caller's included:** rows, objects, local files, the users and their sessions all go, the plan, seats, usage ledger and billing events stay, and the browser leaves through the ordinary sign-out (`POST /api/v1/auth/logout`, then the portal's logout URL or `/sign-in`). Once the erase succeeds the zone shows `settings-reset-erased` (`apps/web/components/settings-erase-account.tsx:277`, `role="status"`) and the red button is disabled until the browser has left (`:268`). Off a hosted build the route answers `403 tenant_reset_disabled`; for a member who is not the owner (an `admin` too), `403 tenant_reset_forbidden`, answered **before** the word is read; for any word but `DELETE` from an owner, `400 invalid_request`; and, **after** the word is checked, `409 tenant_erase_shared_partner` when the server holds more than one company under that partner (SR-163 review round; the panel then shows `settings.reset.eraseAccountShared` and nothing is deleted, because the erase below is partner-wide and scoping it to one company is still owed). That ordering is the safe probe: POST `{ scope: "tenant", confirm: "not-the-word" }` with the `x-agentforge-transport: web` and `x-agentforge-csrf` headers — a non-owner gets 403, an owner gets 400, and nothing is deleted either way. **Destructive: same rule as `settings-reset-all-submit`, and worse** — it signs everybody out of a real company. Prove the two-click flow and the enable flip, then press `settings-reset-cancel`; never press submit on anything but a throwaway tenant on a throwaway hosted instance.
- `settings-reset-pending` (0.14.26) is the banner the same card shows while a full reset is queued (`GET /api/v1/settings` → `resetPending: true`): `settings-reset-pending-cancel` sends `DELETE /api/v1/settings/reset` and swaps to `settings-reset-pending-cancelled`; `settings-reset-pending-restart` (packaged only) relaunches with the wipe. A refused relaunch (update installing, already exiting, forbidden sender) shows `settings-reset-restart-needed` with the reason instead of `settings-reset-restarting`.
- `auth-account` and `auth-signout` (hosted only) are the signed-in row under the settings form (`apps/web/components/account-session-row.tsx:96`, `:118`; rendered at `settings-page.tsx:587`, the plan panel follows at `:589`). The row renders nothing unless `useSession()` is signed in, so a desk has count 0. Its walk is [login.md](./login.md).

## Gotchas

- **A field the save does not read is named back, not refused (SH-002, 2026-10-11).** `POST /api/v1/settings` still answers 200 for a body that carries a name it does not read (`openaiKey` for `openaiApiKey`, say), applies nothing for that name, and adds `ignoredFields: ["openaiKey"]` to the answer. Names only, at most 20 of them. The UI never sends such a name, so `ignoredFields` is absent on every Settings save and on onboarding; a count of 0 for it on a UI drive is the pass. Do not prove the report by POSTing a typo from a console or a script: `SKILL.md` forbids a settings POST as a substitute for the UI. The report is proven by the `POST /api/v1/settings in server mode` and `unknownSettingsFields (SH-002)` blocks in `packages/host/src/handlers/settings.test.ts`.
- Saving probes `GET /v1/models` against `resolvedGatewayBaseUrl()` (`packages/host/src/gateway-gate.ts:340-341`): the pinned Toko Token gateway, or a packaged brand flavor's own gateway (Kemenkeu AI, AIHub Metranet; `packages/core/src/gateway/pinned.ts:72-81`). The public Nultron build is always the pinned one. A missing key is a product 400, not a harness bug.
- **The two status lines can disagree on webdev.** `runtime-status` follows the saved key (`resolveRuntimeMode`), `settings-gateway-status` follows the gate, and the gate short-circuits to `stub` whenever the env pins `AGENTFORGE_RUNTIME=stub` (`apps/web/.env.local` does). Driven 2026-09-23 on `:3000` (process started with the env pin): `Status: Live · Gateway key saved` above a gateway row of `No key saved` and `Re-check`, with a live send succeeding, and Chat's `chat-key-status` read `Connect a key to go live`. Record both lines and `gateway.status`; the packaged app does not set the env and does not show this.
- GET Settings may still embed a this-key snapshot for the slim strip. Full range charts use `GET /api/v1/usage` on the Usage page.
- The raw key never comes back after save. A filled `openai-key` on reload means you are looking at the empty replace-placeholder, not the secret.
- Advanced fields remain in the host store (guard on, tools on) but are not in the GTM UI. Hunting for `injection-guard-bypass` and finding count 0 is a pass.
- `runtime-status` is the user-visible doctor. Trust that text over env `AGENTFORGE_RUNTIME` once a key exists — `resolveRuntimeMode` prefers a saved key.
- Mutating `/api` from a non-localhost Origin is rejected on **webdev**. Packaged has no HTTP API; the renderer uses IPC. Do not doctor :3000 as the app.
- Settings is not a product mode. The rail control is `settings-link`, not `mode-settings`.
- Since 2026-09-17 the endpoint row is gone, not read-only. Hunting for `settings-endpoint` (or `settings-endpoint-reset`) and finding count 0 is the pass; finding either is a regression. Older runs and the 0.14.21 changelog describe an **Endpoint URL** line — that is history, not the current UI.
- The gateway status row and the `settings-reset` Start-over card sit on the same page. Reading the verdict is free; `settings-gateway-recheck` is a live probe. Do not confuse a rejected-key row with a broken page.
- **`settings-reset-key-submit`, `settings-reset-all-submit` and `settings-reset-tenant-submit` are destructive and must not be clicked on the operator's desk unless asked** — the same rule as `save-settings`. Sign-out forgets the gateway key on every desk; fresh install deletes every thread, desk, knowledge base, and generated file, and there is no undo. Typing `RESET` into `settings-reset-all-confirm-name` is safe on its own; the POST only fires on submit. `settings-reset-tenant-submit` deletes every person in a hosted company and signs them all out — the one control here with no throwaway-desk equivalent on webdev, so drive it only against faked answers (as above) or a throwaway hosted tenant. Drive the full wipe on a throwaway `AGENTFORGE_DATA_DIR`, never on the operator's install.
- With a key saved the gateway card has **two** inputs, not one. A recipe that asserts "the only input is the key" fails on `settings-edit-turn-cap`. The endpoint assertion that still holds is the narrower one: no input's *value* contains `tokotokenai`.
- **`save-settings` with the key field blank keeps the saved key (since 2026-10-06, both products).** `onSubmit` sends `settingsSaveBody(…)`, which leaves `openaiApiKey` out unless somebody typed in `openai-key` (`apps/web/components/settings-page.tsx:107`, used at `:300`).
