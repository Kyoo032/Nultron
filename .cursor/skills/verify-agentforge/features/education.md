# Education

Education is a work mode on the Default rail. Opening it with no task shows four plain cards: Lesson, Quiz, A page, and Show. The rail chevron (`education-tasks-toggle`, word `tasks` / `tugas`) lists the same four and starts closed. Each task is `/education?task=`. An unknown task is the lesson. The lesson that appears is edited by clicking words on the slide. Save and shape tools sit in More (`education-more`). A selected shape gets a small toolbar on the slide. None of these writes with a model, but on a desk with a live key the quiz still reaches the gateway: `POST /api/v1/education/exam` embeds the topic to find passages (see Gotchas). The chooser shows the presenting mascot only while the header stage (`buddy-stage-header`) is not on screen; see the chooser bullet. Map: [`docs/internal/maps/education.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/education.md).

## Sub-features

- `education-rail` reaches `/education` from `mode-education`. The row is one card (`RailItem`'s `trailing` slot, `apps/web/components/app-rail.tsx:354-372`): `rail-education-mode` wraps it, and inside it one `[data-rail-row]` holds the `mode-education` link with `education-tasks-toggle` at its right edge (checked on `:3000` 2026-10-01: 32px high at a 1000px window, 28px at a window **height** of 940px or less, `globals.css:698-718`). On `/education` the chevron can open `rail-education-tasks` with four rows: `education-task-lesson`, `education-task-quiz`, `education-task-page`, `education-task-show`. Off `/education` the chevron is `disabled` and `aria-expanded="false"`. The list starts closed, including on Education itself.
- `education-chooser` is `/education` with no `task` query. `education-guide-lead` reads "What do you want to do?" (`Anda ingin melakukan apa?` on `id`). `education-guide-choices` holds `education-guide-lesson`, `education-guide-quiz`, `education-guide-page`, and `education-guide-show`, each a label and one line. The chooser shows the presenting mascot (`data-placement="empty"`, drawn by `ModeIllustration`, `apps/web/components/education-studio.tsx:373`) only while the header stage is not on screen. At a header 60rem wide or more, `buddy-stage-header` (`ModeHeader`, `apps/web/components/mode-header.tsx:66-73`) shows Nultron in the `presenting` pose (`MASCOT_MODE_HOME.education`, `apps/web/lib/mascot-states.ts:114`) and the chooser draws only the flat scene (`MascotSlot` returns nothing while staged). `education-guide-change` on a task screen returns here.
- `education-lesson` is `?task=lesson`, also the screen for an unknown task. There is no empty mascot card. The topic field is `education-lesson-topic` and Create is `education-lesson-draft`. The prompt strip is sticky at the top of the task until a deck exists. Drafting mounts the simple Presentation stage (`presentations-editor`) on its title slide (`presentations-slide-title`, `presentations-edit-title`); `presentations-edit-heading` exists only once a content slide is opened (a `presentations-filmstrip-slide` at index 1 or more, `presentation-preview.tsx:562`, `:643-650`). There is no `presentations-properties`. The add-shape buttons are in More, in the row `presentations-shape-toolbar` (count 1, visible once More is open, not inside `presentations-editor`). Fill, stroke, duplicate, and delete appear on `presentations-selection-toolbar` only while a shape is selected. Arrow keys nudge by 1 percent. `education-save-deck` is in More and stores the deck with the presentation decks. The host checks the four-slide shape and retries a thin draft once.
- `education-exam` is `?task=quiz`. The first screen is the topic field `education-exam-topic` and `education-exam-generate`. There is no `education-exam-empty`. The sheet is `education-exam-sheet`: numbered `education-exam-item`, lettered `education-exam-choice`, and `education-exam-answer`. An empty knowledge base still returns a readable item and no citation line. When a source was retrieved, the citation includes that Knowledge source id.
- `education-book` is `?task=page` (`education-book-file`, `education-book-read`, `education-book-text`). The empty-desk mascot and scene are only in the chooser (`ModeIllustration`, `apps/web/components/education-studio.tsx:373`), not on this task, but `buddy-stage-header` appears on every Education screen, this one included, when the header is 60rem wide or more. It reads a PNG page or a PDF on this machine. A scan is not sent away, and the read is not retried.
- `education-presenter` is `?task=show`. With no lesson in memory (a fresh load) the desk is `education-presenter-needs-lesson`: the sentence "Create a lesson first. Then you can show it." (`Buat pelajaran dulu. Setelah itu Anda bisa menampilkannya.`) and `education-presenter-create-lesson`, a primary link to `?task=lesson` reading "Create a lesson" (`Buat pelajaran`). Nothing else is drawn under the header except the `education-guide-change` link (`education-studio.tsx:397-405`): no `education-more`, no `education-presenter-build`, no sticky prompt strip. There is no `education-presenter-empty`. With a lesson open, `education-presenter-build` is the one primary button until the stage appears. Then `education-presenter-stage` shows the avatar (`education-presenter-avatar`) and the current line (`education-presenter-cue`). Line picks (`education-presenter-cue-pick`) and the spoken script (`education-presenter-dub`) are in More. It does not render a video file. A spoken line that is not on a slide is retried once.
- `education-error` is the alert shown above the task when a local route fails (`apps/web/components/education-studio.tsx:361-369`). It is absent while nothing has failed.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose Education on the left rail (`mode-education`). A desk that already has it shows the tab. A fresh first-run desk starts with Research, Images, Videos and Presentation only, so turn this one on first (Workspaces, Edit on the desk). With no task chosen, the four cards are the first thing on the page.
- Open a task from a card, or open the rail word `tasks` and pick a row.
- Open `http://127.0.0.1:3000/education` when the tab is unlocked. `?task=quiz` opens the quiz. `?task=nope` opens the lesson.

## Driving it with the Nultron harness

Preconditions:

- Doctor exits 0.
- `mode-education` is visible on Default.
- Stub runtime. Do not generate a Presentation or a Document as a stand-in.

- **Chooser.** Click `mode-education`. URL matches `/education` with no `task`. `education-guide-choices` has four links. `education-lesson-topic` is not visible. `education-tasks-toggle` is enabled, `aria-expanded="false"`, and its visible word is `tasks`. `rail-education-tasks` has count 0 until the chevron is pressed.
- **Rail.** Press `education-tasks-toggle`. Four rows appear: `education-task-lesson`, `education-task-quiz`, `education-task-page`, `education-task-show`. Press it again and the rows go away while you stay on `/education`.
- **Lesson.** Open `education-guide-lesson` or `education-task-lesson`. URL contains `task=lesson`. `education-lesson-topic` and `education-lesson-draft` are visible. `education-lesson-empty` and `education-hint` count 0. There is no `education-tab-lesson`. Type a topic and click `education-lesson-draft`. `presentations-editor` and `presentations-filmstrip` appear. `presentations-shape-toolbar` is not inside `presentations-editor`; it is the add-shape row inside More. Open a content slide and change `presentations-edit-heading`. Open More and click `presentations-add-ellipse`. Select the shape: `presentations-selection-toolbar` shows. ArrowRight nudges `data-x` by 1. `presentations-shape-duplicate` copies it. `presentations-shape-remove` in `presentations-selection-toolbar` removes the copy (Delete or Backspace does the same, `presentation-preview.tsx:348`, the handler spans `:332-391`; it ignores events from inputs and contenteditable (`:337-343`), so blur the heading first). Click `education-save-deck`. `education-deck-saved` appears.
- **Quiz.** Open `?task=quiz` (card `education-guide-quiz` or row `education-task-quiz`). `education-exam-topic` is visible. Click `education-exam-generate`: it is `disabled` while the topic is blank (`education-studio.tsx:324`), so type one (the topic state is shared with the lesson field, `:142`, so a lesson topic carries over). `education-exam-sheet` shows at least one numbered `education-exam-item`, lettered `education-exam-choice` rows, and `education-exam-answer`, including when the desk has no indexed source.
- **A page.** Open `?task=page`. Set `education-book-file` to a PNG page drawn by the local face (the letters `LOCAL PAGE SCAN`) and click `education-book-read`. `education-book-text` contains those letters. A `.txt` file stays on the page with a local message and no outbound reader.
- **Show, no lesson.** Load `/education?task=show` fresh (the drafted lesson lives in component state, so a reload or a new tab starts without one). `education-presenter-needs-lesson` is visible with the sentence above, `education-presenter-create-lesson` is visible and its `href` is `/education?task=lesson`, and `education-more`, `education-presenter-build` and `education-presenter-stage` have count 0. Click `education-presenter-create-lesson`: URL gains `task=lesson` and `education-lesson-topic` is visible. On `id` the sentence and the link read in Bahasa Indonesia; testids do not move. Unit proof: `apps/web/lib/education-show-empty.test.tsx` (server-rendered; the click is not covered there).
- **Show.** With the lesson deck still open, open `?task=show`, then `education-presenter-build`. `education-presenter-stage` shows `education-presenter-avatar` and one `education-presenter-cue`. Open More: `education-presenter-dub` is there. There is no video file. The avatar does not show a motion name. `education-guide-change` returns to the chooser.
- **Unknown task.** Open `/education?task=nope`. The lesson screen is showing (`education-lesson-topic`), not the chooser and not an error.
- **Locale (id).** With the desk on `id`, the rail reads `Pendidikan`, the chevron word is `tugas`, the chooser lead is `Anda ingin melakukan apa?`, and Create reads `Buat`. Testids do not move.

## Gotchas

- The lesson editor is `PresentationPreview` with `variant="simple"`. Its testids stay `presentations-*` on this page. Shape tools and save are inside `education-more-details`, which stays open after a click because the `<details>` element is not remounted.
- `education-presenter-build` is the primary button on Show until a plan exists, and only when a lesson is already in memory. After that, the same testid is the quiet button inside More. Only one of them is mounted. Switching away from Show does not drop the lesson.
- The book reader does not use the document converter's scan refusal as the owner-facing result. A scan comes back as a local empty reading.
- Exam questions are assembled from retrieved passages, or from a fixed empty-base item. They are not graded and they are not a model paper. Each filled item carries the Knowledge `sourceId`, and the citation string includes that id plus a sentence from the source. A citation that does not resolve is retried once.
- **The quiz bills on a keyed desk (driven 2026-10-01).** `handlePostEducationExam` calls `retrieveChunks(tenant, topic || "lesson", 6)` (`packages/host/src/handlers/education.ts:43`, inside the handler at `:36`), which embeds the topic through the live gateway key; embeddings are on the hot path of every knowledge read. On a shared keyed desk answer `POST /api/v1/education/exam` in the page with `page.route` (fulfil with the shape the sheet expects) and record the real-host quiz as "not driven (paid)". The lesson, page and show routes are local. See SKILL.md **Keys**.
- The presenter plan sets `rendered` false. Avatar motion is a placement record (`data-motion`), not a moving picture. Cue text has to be a line already on the slide.
- The lesson, the quiz, and the show retry once. A page does not. The page read stays on this machine. The retry is local code (`packages/host/src/education/harness.ts`), not a model call, so it costs no tokens and leaves the response's `harness` object (`skill`, `phases`, `attempts`, `checked`) as its only trace.
- The chevron starts closed on `/education` itself. Leaving Education forgets that it was open. Nothing about it is stored.
- **More is drawn only once it holds something (2026-10-02).** `education-more` is drawn when `hasMore = Boolean(outline) || ...` (`education-studio.tsx:340`), and `outline` is shared across tasks: it has count 0 only on a desk with no lesson in memory (the lesson desk before a deck exists, the quiz desk before a quiz, a page, Show with no lesson), but once a lesson is in memory it also draws on the quiz desk before a quiz is written, on the page task and on Show (holding Save); it appears with the deck (Save and the shape tools), with a written quiz, and with a presenter plan. Hold that when a recipe says "open More": make the lesson first. The prompt strip around it is drawn only while it has a control or More in it, so Show with no lesson has no sticky bar either.
- A hidden Education pane (keep-alive) is not the chooser just because the visible route has no `task` query. The studio keeps the last task until `/education` is showing again.
- Smaller facts: once a deck exists the Create controls (`education-lesson-topic`, `education-lesson-draft`) move into More (`education-studio.tsx:545`), so asserting `education-lesson-draft` visible after drafting fails until More is open; a second Create replaces the outline but does not clear a stale presenter plan (`onLesson` never calls `setPresenter(null)`), while editing a heading or bullet clears the plan and the saved flag (`:418-422`). On task screens the header outcome line is `expected-inputs` (lesson "A lesson you can change.", quiz "Questions you can read.", page "Read a picture or a PDF on this computer.", show "Show the lesson with a voice."); `aria-current="true"` sits on `education-task-lesson` even on the chooser; `education-exam-choice` carries `data-correct`; `education-more-details` is the `<details>` and `education-presenter-layout` a wrapper. Quiz output is at most 5 items: a filled item has two choices ("The sentence in the citation" is correct) and a citation `<sourceId> · <name>: <first sentence, 180 characters>`; with no indexed source it is one item "No indexed passage was available. Add a source, then generate this exam again." with the choices "Add a source" and "Invent a fact that was not saved" and no citation line. A lesson is titled "Lesson: <topic>" (topic cut at 200 characters) with the headings "What this lesson is for", "One worked example", "Check yourself" and "What to remember", so the filmstrip has 5 slides; Show gives 8 cues for a fresh lesson ("Slide 1. What this lesson is for", then the bullet, per slide) and one `education-presenter-cue-pick` per cue (aria-pressed, in More); the header buddy starts `wave` for 1400 ms, then `presenting`. Shapes: nine `presentations-add-<rectangle|rounded|ellipse|triangle|line|arrow|star|callout|text>`; adding one selects it at once (`addKind`, `:275-285`) so the selection toolbar shows with no extra click and a fresh shape has `data-x` 4; ArrowRight nudges `data-x` by 1 and Shift+Arrow by 5; duplicate is disabled at 24 shapes per slide (`:623`); the toolbar also holds `presentations-fill`, `presentations-stroke` and `presentations-shape-text`. `education-save-deck` writes a new `deck_<hex>.json` on every click (`presentation-decks.ts:37-44`, no delete route), and `education-deck-saved` renders only on the lesson task. The book reader reads back only a PNG made by `renderPagePng("LOCAL PAGE SCAN")` (8-bit grayscale, colour type 0, unfiltered, `page-face.ts:150`, `:166`): any other PNG gives "The scan stayed on this machine. Local reading found no letters in the page face. Nothing was sent away.", a `.txt` gives "This reader takes a PNG page or a PDF. Nothing was sent away.", and `education-book-read` is disabled until a file is chosen (the `accept` attribute does not stop `setInputFiles`).
