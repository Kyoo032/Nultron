# Shell scroll

The desk is the viewport. The document does not scroll, and each page has one scrollport: Chat and Edit fill the pane and scroll inside it, and every other work mode plus Settings, Knowledge, Workspaces, Usage and Channels scroll the pane. Work modes stay mounted, so a rail click keeps `scrollTop`. Reading columns stay near 768px on Education, onboarding and the Finance chooser only (`--content-max`); Chat has no reading cap (`apps/web/src/pages/chat-page.tsx:14-21` is the modes fallback, where the pane owns the whole width; this build is `chat-first`, so `AgentChatPane` renders instead, `apps/web/components/agent-chat-pane.tsx:83`; `message-list` is `w-full` with `px-[var(--chat-gutter)]`, `chat-session.tsx:496`; `chat-home` measured 1192px in a 1440px window); studios grow on a wide window and stop short of the desk edge. Map: [`docs/internal/maps/shell-rail-and-workspaces.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/shell-rail-and-workspaces.md) §2. The 480px rail collapse is [rail.md](./rail.md), not this file.

Product: both · Instance: main :3000

## Sub-features

- `shell-one-scroller` — `body` is `overflow: hidden` and `document.scrollingElement` does not move. `[data-testid="app-main-panel"]` is `overflow: hidden` (there is no `#app-main-panel` id: select it by the testid, `apps/web/components/app-shell.tsx:37`). On a scrolling page the only desk scroller is the pane around the page (the visible `overflow-y: auto` ancestor of `legal-studio`, `finance-studio`, or `settings-form`). The rail `<nav>` is the rail's own scroller and is not a second desk bar.
- `shell-backdrop` (2026-10-01) — `desk-backdrop` (`aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none`, `position: absolute; inset: 0`, `apps/web/app/globals.css:1303-1308`, with the no-overflow rationale at `:1294-1302`) is the first child of `app-main-panel` (`apps/web/components/app-shell.tsx:38`). It holds the pointer's light, the dot grid and the click rings: decoration, not a scroller. Nothing in its rule sets `overflow` (the canvas, which is `overflow: hidden`, clips what the layers hold past its edge), so it is never a desk scroller and adds no scroll width to the page; the rule's own comment says a layer that clipped for itself would read as a hidden horizontal scroller on every page. Written from source on 2026-10-01, not driven.
- `shell-fill-chat` — `message-list` is Chat's scroller (`overflow-y: auto`) and it fills the desk pane. `composer` stays at the bottom of `chat-home` while that list moves. The scrollbar is the pane's right edge. On the modes fallback a thread's list also carries `chat-thread-ledge` (6.5rem of bottom padding, `apps/web/app/globals.css:2069-2071`, `chat-session.tsx:496`), so its last message ends above the buddy standing on the composer's top edge instead of behind it; the chat-first shell (`SHELL_LAYOUT`, `packages/core/src/agents/product-modes.ts:29`) passes `shell` and gets an ordinary gap instead; the empty Chat's list has no such class.
- `shell-fill-edit` — `edit-studio` fills `app-main-panel` (`h-full`, `overflow: hidden`). With no project open the inner scroller depends on the pane's width (`EditEmptyState`, `apps/web/components/edit-empty-state.tsx:32-45`): under 40rem it is the empty state's root wrapper (`overflow-y: auto`, `:42`) and `edit-project-list` is just a tall child; from 40rem it is `edit-project-list` itself (`:45`). The page does not grow a bar.
- `shell-scroll-keep` — a work mode's pane `scrollTop` is the same after `mode-chat` and back. Account routes unmount, so Settings does not keep its place.
- `shell-stream-follow` — after a send, `message-list` sits within 64px of the end (`apps/web/lib/stick-to-bottom.ts`). Wheeling up more than 64px leaves it there. The next send jumps back to the end. `new-chat-link` resets the list to the top.
- `shell-wide-lanes` — viewport width picks the lane. `--content-max` is 736px at 1920 and 768px at 2560 and 3440. `--content-stage` (`finance-studio`) is 1600px at 1920, 1792px at 2560, and 1984px at 3440. Only `--content-stage` bounds an element on the pages this recipe measures (`finance-studio-view.tsx:118-119`); `--content-max` bounds nothing on Chat, Finance or Edit (its readers are `education-studio.tsx:375`, `onboarding-screen.tsx:241` and `finance-steps/finance-guide.tsx:13`; `--composer-max` is declared at `globals.css:162` and never used). The values themselves are right (`globals.css:476-514`; read on 2026-10-05 at 1920, 2560 and 3440).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open Chat. A long thread scrolls in the message column. The composer stays on screen. A reply follows the bottom until you scroll up to read.
- Open Legal or Finance, scroll down, open Chat, then come back. The page is where you left it.
- On a wide monitor the Chat pane takes the whole width (there is no reading cap on Chat, see above; `message-list` is as wide as `chat-home`). Finance uses more of the desk and still has space on both sides. Edit uses the whole desk.

## Driving it with the Nultron harness

Preconditions:

- Doctor exits 0 on stub webdev. Do not start a second server while `:3000` answers.
- Drive with client rail clicks. `page.goto` reloads and starts every pane at the top; that is not a fail.
- Wait until `.page-enter` has finished (about 560ms: `--motion-5`, `globals.css:220`; it also replays on every un-hide, display:none to block restarts the animation, so wait after every rail return, not only the first) before measuring widths. Mid-animation the column is scaled down.
- Leave Finance, Market and Education (the Education sub-list is the same `RailSubmenu`, `rail-submenu.tsx:220-221`) chevrons closed. An open submenu is its own list scroller.

- **One scroller.** On `/legal`, `body` computed `overflow` is `hidden` and `document.scrollingElement.scrollTop` is 0. Exactly one visible desk element has `overflow-y` of `auto` or `scroll` and `scrollHeight > clientHeight`: the pane that contains `legal-studio`. Repeat on `/settings` with `settings-form`. The rail `<nav>` may also overflow on a short window; do not count it as a desk scroller. `desk-backdrop` is not one either: its computed `overflow` is `visible`, so it never matches `auto` or `scroll`, and `document.documentElement.scrollWidth - innerWidth` stays 0 with it on the page.
- **Chat fills.** On a thread long enough to overflow, wheel `message-list`. Its `scrollTop` changes. `composer`'s bottom stays inside `app-main-panel`. `document.scrollingElement.scrollTop` stays 0.
- **Edit fills.** On `/edit` with no project, `edit-studio`'s width is within 8px of `app-main-panel`'s. Which element scrolls follows the pane width, divided by 16 for rem (read it on the grandparent of `edit-project-list`): under 40rem (a pane under 640 px, so a window under about 880 px with the default rail; a 768 px window gives 520 px) the scroller is that root wrapper and `edit-project-list` does not scroll by itself; from 40rem (1024 px gives 776 px, 1440 px gives 1192 px) `edit-project-list` is the scroller (`overflow-y: auto`, `scrollHeight` > `clientHeight`). Create projects until the list is taller than the pane, or nothing overflows. At both sizes the document does not move.
- **Keep.** On `/legal`, set the pane's `scrollTop` to 300 (skip if `scrollHeight` cannot reach it; lengthen the window narrower instead). Click `mode-chat`, then `agent-legal` (then `agent-open-studio`). The same pane's `scrollTop` is still 300, and `legal-studio` was never unmounted (`hidden` while you were on Chat). **On the first return it can read more than you set:** driven 2026-10-01 at 1440 x 560, the Legal pane read 380, not 300, after one Chat detour (scroll anchoring moved it while the studio un-hid), and the Finance pane at 180 held exactly. So either settle (wait out `.page-enter`, about 700 ms) and detour twice before reading, or assert `scrollTop >= 300` rather than `=== 300`. Repeat with `agent-finance` (then `agent-open-studio`) at 180, detour through `settings-link`, and come back. **Finance does not hold exactly (known product bug, driven 2026-10-05):** it loses 1 px on each detour. Compare Finance with a tolerance of a few px and record the drift, and keep Legal as the exact check (it held at 380 across two detours the same day). See Gotchas.
- **Follow.** On stub Chat, send four short turns (`composer-text`, `composer-send`). When `assistant-live` is gone, `message-list` is within 64px of the end (`scrollHeight - scrollTop - clientHeight <= 64`). Wheel up so that distance is greater than 64 and wait; it must not jump. Send once more: it returns to the end. Click `new-chat-link`: `scrollTop` is 0. On an empty Chat at 1280×800, `message-list` `scrollHeight - clientHeight` is 0 (measured 2026-10-01 on the new hero, the character beside a speech bubble, before that day's tier fix; also 0 at 1280×860, but 35px at a 760px window, 15px at 780px and 6px at 901px, which is the defect the tier fix targets. The fix is in source now (`globals.css:1738-1833`: the `.chat-empty-fit` container at `:1742`, tiers at max-height 1040 `:1750`, 960 `:1756`, 900 `:1763`, 860 `:1782` and 800 `:1806`), so re-read the sweep; see [chat.md](./chat.md), "Hero", Room, for the full height sweep).
- **Wide.** Set the viewport to 1920×1080, 2560×1440, and 3440×1440. At each, read `--content-max` and `--content-stage` on `documentElement` and assert the table in `shell-wide-lanes`. `message-list` width matches `chat-home` (the px-6 inset is inside the scroller, not a centred column). `finance-studio` width equals `--content-stage` (±2px) and is less than the panel. `edit-studio` still fills the panel. On chat-first the rail stays the icon column (`data-rail="min"`, no labels and no collapse; driven 2026-10-14 at 1280). Labelled collapse is the `"modes"` fallback in [rail.md](./rail.md).
- **Evidence.** Screenshots of Chat, Finance, and Edit at the three widths, plus the Legal scrollTop before and after the Chat detour, under `evidence/shell-scroll/<run-id>/`.

## Gotchas

- **Known product bug: the Finance pane loses 1 px of `scrollTop` per rail detour (driven 2026-10-05 at 1440 x 560, `/finance`, pane `scrollHeight` 646, `clientHeight` 544).** Set to 60, then `mode-chat` and back to `agent-finance` (then `agent-open-studio`) (about 1.1 s each): 59, then 58, then 57, and 56 after a detour through `settings-link`. Set to 180, the pane clamps to 102 and comes back 101 (the same drift, with the real gate). Legal held exactly in the same run (380, 380, 380), so this is not the keep-alive as a whole. The cause is not found. Log it in `docs/internal/unreleased.md` as a finding, not a skill fix; do not loosen the Legal check to hide it. Evidence: `evidence/shell-scroll/2026-10-05-maintain/finance-keep-drift.md`. One unverified lead from a source read (2026-10-06): each return restarts `.page-enter` while the pane is un-hiding, so scroll anchoring and clamping run mid-animation, which would also explain Legal reading 380 and not 300 on a first return; it does not explain why Finance differs from Legal.
- A full load resets scroll. Only a rail click (or another in-app navigation) is the keep check.
- Settings, Knowledge, Workspaces, Usage, and Channels mount with the route. Leaving them drops `scrollTop`. The keep check is a work mode.
- Sending re-pins. Scrolling up and then pressing `composer-send` jumping to the end is the pass, not a yank bug. A yank is the list moving while you did not send.
- Hidden work modes stay in the DOM. Assert the visible pane, not "count of `legal-studio` is 0" after you have visited Legal.
- The rail `<nav>` scrolls when the window is shorter than the mode list. That bar is the rail. An open Finance or Market submenu adds another list scroller; close it before counting desk scrollers.
- At 1280 the stage token is wider than the desk, so `finance-studio` shrinks to the panel. The "narrower than the panel" check is for 1920, 2560, and 3440.
- Edit is supposed to be edge to edge inside the desk. Failing it for not using `--content-max` is the wrong assertion.
- `desk-backdrop` and its layers (`.desk-spot`, `.desk-grid`, `.desk-ripple`) are decoration inside `app-main-panel`. Skip them when you count scrollers or measure content, and select desk scrollers by computed `overflow-y`, not by "an element with a big `scrollHeight`". On the modes fallback a thread's `message-list` is longer than its messages by the 6.5rem `chat-thread-ledge` padding; that is the buddy's room, not a bug (the chat-first shell has no ledge).
- Smaller facts: `chat-empty` (`chat-launcher.tsx:93`) is the empty Chat's own testid; the Edit "create projects" step has the handles `edit-starter`, `edit-project-name` and `edit-new-project` (`edit-empty-state.tsx:54`, `:71`, `:76`) and creating a project mutates the desk (throwaway desks only); `html[data-shell]` makes the dot-grid drift smooth (12 s, linear) on webdev and `steps(8)` over 19.2 s when packaged (`globals.css:243-245`, `:319-325`) and changes nothing for scrollers or `scrollWidth`; a hidden pane is the `hidden` attribute plus the `hidden` class plus `aria-hidden` (`work-mode-keep-alive.tsx:87`). `README.md` says "Presentation preview is two columns from 1600px": no 1600px breakpoint exists in `presentation*.tsx` (`presentation-preview.tsx:502` is `grid-cols-1` for the simple variant; the 1600px rules are gallery cards, `example-gallery.tsx:22`), and Presentation does not read `--content-stage` (only `finance-studio-view`, `meeting-studio` and `channels-page` do), so that claim is probably stale and belongs to [presentations.md](./presentations.md).
- **Driven 2026-10-05 on `:3000` (keyed; maintain3), no send.** At 1440 x 560 `/legal` and `/settings` each had `body` `overflow: hidden`, `document.scrollingElement.scrollTop` 0, exactly one `overflow-y: auto` scroller above the studio, no horizontal page overflow and `desk-backdrop` `overflow: visible`; setting the Legal pane's `scrollTop` to 300 and detouring through Chat and back kept it at 290 or more; `/finance` at 1920 x 1080, 2560 x 1440 and 3440 x 1440 read `--content-max` 736px / 768px / 768px and `--content-stage` 1600px / 1792px / 1984px; `chat-home` was 1192px wide at 1440.
