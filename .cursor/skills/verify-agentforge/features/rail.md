# Rail

The rail is the left column every screen shares: the product mark and desk switcher on top, then three labelled groups — Converse (Chat plus the session list), Make (one tab per `productMode` the current desk owns; the English label is `Make`, Bahasa Indonesia is `Buat`), Account (Knowledge Base, Channels, Workspaces, Usage, Settings) — and a footer that is one aligned row of 32px icon squares: `theme-toggle`, optionally `desk-buddy-chip` (only while the desk buddy has no room for a body, see `rail-buddy`), the updates icon where it exists, and `rail-collapse` / `rail-expand`. The Account group pins to the bottom edge of the scrolling mode list (`rail-account-group`) when the list overflows, so Settings stays on screen at any window height while the modes scroll behind it; when the list fits, the group sits under the last row and the buddy's dock fills the space beneath it. Rows are 32px, and 28px at a window height of 940px or less. Theme and collapse are always there. The updates icon is packaged public Nultron only; webdev and the hosted web render none. Labels on the rows stay visible down to 480px; at 480px or narrower the rail starts collapsed. Footer words live in `aria-label` / `title` only, except the updates button's label while a release is pending. It renders two chrome states from one component: expanded (default 232px, resizable 168–360) and collapsed (a fixed 68px icon column). What it shows is not configuration — it is the current workspace's `productModes` run through `resolveWorkspaceModes`, so a desk that hides a mode also makes its URL unreachable. The bullet names below (`rail-groups`, `rail-modes`, `rail-sessions`, …) are sub-feature names for this file, not testids; the testids are the ones quoted inside each bullet. Map: [`docs/internal/maps/shell-rail-and-workspaces.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/shell-rail-and-workspaces.md).

## Sub-features

- `rail-groups` is header, Converse (`rail.groupConverse` = `Converse` / `Percakapan`), Make (`rail.groupJobs` = `Make` / `Buat`, omitted entirely when the desk has no non-chat mode), Account (`rail.groupAccount`, wrapped in `rail-account-group`). Group text exists only when expanded — collapsed renders a 1px divider instead.
- `rail-modes` is one `mode-${href.slice(1)}` per visible mode, in catalog order (`mode-chat`, `mode-documents`, `mode-research`, `mode-finance`, `mode-data`, `mode-market`, `mode-legal`, `mode-meeting`, `mode-images`, `mode-videos`, `mode-music`, `mode-edit`, `mode-presentations`, `mode-education`). Default shows all **fourteen**. `mode-agents` and `mode-channels` count is always 0. No row carries a `New` badge — 0.15.0 deleted the mechanism (`NEW_BADGE_UNTIL`, `newBadgeOn()`, `rail.badgeNew`), so `mode-music` reads exactly `Music` and `mode-meeting` exactly `Meeting`. The current row is marked by its fill and `aria-current="page"` only; the left accent stripe is gone (`border-left-width` 0, no `::before`).
- `rail-finance-tasks` is the Finance sub-list: the Finance row is wrapped in `rail-finance-mode` and carries the chevron `finance-tasks-toggle` inside its own card (the link and the chevron share one `[data-rail-row]` wrapper, so the active fill and the hover wash run under both; `mode-finance` stays on the link); on `/finance` the chevron can open `rail-finance-tasks`, inside the indented wrapper `rail-finance-tasks-branch`, with five `finance-task-<id>` rows (closed until pressed) (the English labels are "Explain my numbers", "How long will the cash last?", "Plan versus what happened", "Is this project worth it?" and "Is the business healthy?", `locales/en/finance.json:462-483`; the ids are `brief`, `cashflow`, `budget`, `appraisal`, `ratios`). Off `/finance` the chevron is `disabled` and `aria-expanded="false"`. Rows and task behaviour belong to [finance.md](./finance.md).
- `rail-education-tasks` is the Education sub-list: the Education row is wrapped in `rail-education-mode` and carries the chevron `education-tasks-toggle`; on `/education` the chevron can open `rail-education-tasks`, inside the indented wrapper `rail-education-tasks-branch`, with four `education-task-<id>` rows (closed until pressed): Lesson, Quiz, A page, Show. Off `/education` the chevron is `disabled` and `aria-expanded="false"`. Task screens belong to [education.md](./education.md).
- `rail-submenu-chevron` — the three chevrons (`finance-tasks-toggle`, `rail-market-specialists-toggle`, `education-tasks-toggle`) show a short visible word (`tasks` / `specialists` / `tasks`, `tugas` / `spesialis` / `tugas` in `id`) and keep the full `Show tasks` / `Show specialists` phrase (`Hide …` while open) in `aria-label` and `title` (`apps/web/components/rail-submenu.tsx`). CSS uppercases the word (`uppercase`, `rail-submenu.tsx:117`), so `innerText` reads `TASKS` / `SPECIALISTS` while `textContent` reads `tasks` / `specialists`: read `textContent` or match case-insensitively. The sub-list stays closed until that chevron is pressed, including on the mode's own route. A mode row that reads less than its full name is still the regression to avoid — the short word sits on the chevron, not in place of the mode name.
- `rail-market-specialists` is the Market sub-list's **chrome**: on an expanded rail the Market row is wrapped in `rail-market-mode` and carries `rail-market-specialists-toggle` (the chevron, `aria-expanded`); under it `rail-market-specialists-branch` is the indented wrapper with the hairline guide and `rail-market-specialists` is the `role="group"` scroll container holding one `rail-market-specialist-<id>` row per agent. The block belongs to `/market`: it starts closed even there, the chevron opens it while you stay on the route, and off `/market` the rows are gone and the chevron is `disabled` — nothing about that state is stored, so there is no rail-prefs key to assert. The rows are height-adaptive and carry `data-overflowing` while they are cut off (four at 720 and 768, about seven at 900, all eleven from 1008 up); every rail row is `shrink-0`, so the nav scrolls rather than squashing rows to fit the block in. Which desk a row opens, what it swaps, and every `market-*` testid belong to [market.md](./market.md); this file owns only "the block is there, and it disappears when the rail collapses".
- `rail-sessions` is the CONVERSE session block's **chrome**: expanded, `rail-thread-list` wraps `new-chat-link`, holds up to four `thread-item` rows (`RAIL_RECENT_THREADS = 4`, `apps/web/lib/thread-groups.ts:2`), and shows `threads-see-all` when the host returned more than four and `rail-thread-error` when the list load or a delete failed. Collapsed, the session rows unmount and `new-chat-link` stays as an icon plus `aria-label`. This is the product's only session list. What a row does — open, delete, the confirm copy, the fork race — belongs to [chat.md](./chat.md).
- `rail-account` is the five fixed links no desk can hide: `mode-knowledge` (`/knowledge`), `channels-link` (`/channels`), `workspaces-link` (`/workspaces`), `usage-link` (`/usage`), `settings-link` (`/settings`). All five carry the same testid in both chrome states (`apps/web/components/app-rail.tsx:405`, `:414`, `:423`, `:432`, `:441`).
- `rail-pinned` is the Account group's placement: `rail-account-group` (`app-rail.tsx:397`, class `rail-pinned`, `position: sticky; bottom: 0`, `apps/web/app/globals.css:672-677`, the `::before` at `:679-688`, `[data-more="true"]` at `:690-696`, the comment at `:666-671`) sits at the bottom edge of the scrolling `<nav>` while the list overflows, so a short window scrolls the Make rows behind it and never the five links. It pins only then: when every row fits, the group stays right under the last row and `desk-buddy-dock` (the next child of the nav, `flex: 1 1 0`, `apps/web/components/desk-buddy.css:8-16`) fills the space beneath it. Its `data-more` is `"true"` while rows are hidden below the nav's visible edge (`apps/web/lib/use-more-below.ts:15-55`) and then a fade and a hairline show above it; `"false"` when everything fits or the nav is scrolled to its end. Rows are 32px with a 24px tile, and 28px with a 22px tile at a window height of 940px or less (`@media (max-height: 940px)`, `globals.css:698-718`, the `@media` line at `:702`).
- `rail-buddy` (2026-10-01) is the desk buddy's place in the rail; what it does, how big it is and what it reacts to are the `desk-buddy*` sub-features and recipes in [mascot.md](./mascot.md). `desk-buddy-dock` is the last child of `nav[data-tour="rail-modes"]`, after `rail-account-group` (`apps/web/components/app-rail.tsx:445`), with `data-view` `body`, `chip` or `none`; `desk-buddy-chip` sits in `rail-footer` beside `theme-toggle` (`app-rail.tsx:462`) and exists only while the view is `chip`. A collapsed rail has none of them: the dock renders `null` and mounts nothing (`apps/web/components/desk-buddy.tsx:188-190`), so the view is `none`. It is `none` on an expanded rail too while another full-body character or a page stage is on screen.
- `rail-brand` is `product-logo` (both states) plus `product-brand` (**expanded only**), which links to the desk's first visible mode. Packaged flavors must not read Nultron — see [desktop-brands.md](./desktop-brands.md).
- `rail-collapse` / `rail-expand` is one button with a flipping testid: `rail-collapse` while expanded, `rail-expand` while collapsed (`apps/web/components/app-rail.tsx:469`). It writes `agentforge-rail-collapsed` (`"1"` / `"0"`) and sets `data-rail="min"` / `"full"` on the `<aside>`. A viewport at `max-width: 480px` starts collapsed and does not write that key when the button is pressed.
- `rail-resize` is the `role="separator"` drag handle on the right edge, expanded only. Pointer drag sets the width; ArrowLeft/ArrowRight move ±8px, Home snaps to 168, End to 360 (the 232 / 168 / 360 / 68 constants are `apps/web/lib/panel-width.ts:3`; the key handling is `panel-resize-handle.tsx:62-82` and the write `use-panel-width.ts:13-20`). It writes `agentforge-rail-width` and `aria-valuenow` tracks it.
- `rail-theme` is `theme-toggle` in the footer. **Default with nothing stored is light** since 0.15.0 (`resolveTheme(null)` → `"light"`, `apps/web/lib/theme.ts`): `<html>` has class `light` and `:root` carries the light values. A stored `dark` adds class `dark` and removes `light`. `aria-label` and `title` read the target mode (`common.theme.dark` = `Dark` on a light desk, `Light` on a dark one); no word is rendered next to the icon in either rail state (`apps/web/components/theme-toggle.tsx:43-45`). The rail follows the theme (`--rail-*` per theme). Type is DM Sans for the UI and for headings (`--font-heading` is `var(--font)`, `globals.css:77`, applied to h1-h6 at `:545-555`); Source Serif 4 is `--font-display` (`:79`), used only by `.chat-bubble-hello` and `.chat-hero-title` (`:2433-2434`, `:2537-2540`). Both families are bundled locally.
- `rail-updates` is the footer updates control: wrapper `app-updates`, button `app-updates-toggle`, then `app-updates-badge` / `app-updates-panel` / `app-updates-status` / `app-updates-check` / `app-updates-install` / `app-updates-close`. It renders only when the product name is Nultron **and** `isElectron()` is true (`apps/web/lib/use-app-updates.ts`). On webdev and the hosted web, `app-updates` has count 0 — there is no disabled check button. Packaged public Nultron shows the icon; Kemenkeu and Metranet still render none. See [desktop.md](./desktop.md).
- `rail-first-paint` is what the rail draws before `GET /api/v1/workspaces` answers (2026-09-29): a browser that has drawn a desk before draws that desk's modes (`localStorage` `agentforge-shell-modes`, `:<tenantId>` on the hosted app); one with nothing stored, a brand-new install, draws Chat and four still skeleton rows under the Make label (`rail-modes-skeleton` wrapping four `rail-modes-skeleton-row`, no `mode-` testid, `aria-busy` on the rail's `<nav>`). Never every mode. The host's answer replaces either, and only it may redirect (`rail-redirect`). Rows that replace the skeleton do not slide in. While the skeleton shows, its four `rail-modes-skeleton-row` rows also match `[data-rail-row]` (`apps/web/components/rail-modes-skeleton.tsx:27`), so count `[data-rail-row]` only after the host's answer.
- `rail-redirect` is the other half of `productModes`: entering a hidden mode's URL lands on the desk's first visible mode, and `/agents` / `/studio` do the same. `/chat`, `/settings`, `/workspaces`, `/usage` and `/knowledge` are never redirected.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The rail is on every screen after onboarding. There is no route for it.
- Webdev: `http://127.0.0.1:3000/chat`. Packaged: the Electron window on Chat.
- Collapse or expand it with the footer button; drag its right edge to resize.
- The desk name under the product name opens the switcher; Knowledge Base, Channels, Workspaces, Usage and Settings are the pinned Account group at the bottom of the rail.

## Driving it with the Nultron harness

Preconditions:

- Doctor exits 0.
- Read `localStorage["agentforge-rail-width"]` and `["agentforge-rail-collapsed"]` **before** touching either, and restore both at the end — they are the operator's chrome, not test state. A throwaway Playwright profile starts empty and needs no restore.

- **Groups (expanded).** With `rail-collapse` visible, the `mode-*` testids are exactly the desk's modes plus `mode-knowledge`. On the operator's existing Default that is all fourteen work modes; on a fresh Personal desk it is Chat, Research, Images, Videos and Presentation (`mode-documents`, `mode-finance`, `mode-data`, `mode-market`, `mode-legal`, `mode-meeting`, `mode-music`, `mode-edit` and `mode-education` all count 0 until the owner turns them on). Collect them by prefix; do not match group headings by text — they are localized.
- **Sessions.** On a wide window, `rail-thread-list` count 1 and `new-chat-link` count 1. On a desk with history, `thread-item` > 0 and `threads-see-all` count 1; on a fresh desk both are 0 and that is the pass. A viewport at 480px or narrower starts collapsed. At 700px the rail is still expanded (the media query collapses only at 480px or narrower, `app-rail.tsx:217-219`, unless the stored `agentforge-rail-collapsed` key is `1`), so `new-chat-link` is 1 and `thread-item` is above 0 on a desk with history (driven 2026-10-05 on `:3000`: `[data-rail]` `full`, `thread-item` 1, `new-chat-link` 1); at 470 px it was `min`. Opening, deleting and the send-too-early race are driven in [chat.md](./chat.md), not here.
- **Collapse.** Click `rail-collapse`. Wait on `rail-expand`. Assert `[data-rail="min"]`, `<aside>` width 68, `localStorage["agentforge-rail-collapsed"] === "1"`, and count 0 for `rail-thread-list`, `rail-resize`, `product-brand` and `desk-buddy-dock`. `workspaces-link`, `workspaces-switcher`, `usage-link`, `settings-link` and `product-logo` stay count 1.
- **Expand.** Click `rail-expand`. Wait on `rail-collapse`. `data-rail` is `full`, width is the stored value, the key reads `"0"`.
- **Resize.** Focus `rail-resize`. `End` → width 360, `Home` → 168, three ArrowRights from 232 → 256. Drag it with the pointer and assert `localStorage["agentforge-rail-width"]` matches the new width. Reload and assert the width survives.
- **Theme.** Read `document.documentElement.className` first and `localStorage["agentforge-theme"]`. With nothing stored, class is `light`. Click `theme-toggle`: class becomes `dark`, key reads `dark`, `aria-label` flips to `Light`. Click again: class `light`, key `light`. There is no `data-theme` attribute. Leave the operator's theme as you found it (a fresh Playwright profile has nothing to restore). Driven 2026-09-23.
- **Footer row.** On webdev and the hosted web, `rail-footer` holds `theme-toggle`, optionally `desk-buddy-chip` (32×32, present only while `desk-buddy-dock` reads `data-view="chip"`), and `rail-collapse` (or `rail-expand`), each 32×32 with empty `innerText`, and `app-updates` has count 0. On packaged public Nultron it also holds `app-updates-toggle`, an icon as well except while a release is available, downloading or ready to install and the rail is expanded: then it widens and shows a label (`apps/web/components/app-updates.tsx:122`, `:161`). Icon colour is the rail text colour (the 0.15.0 fix for dark-on-dark icons: `[data-testid="rail-footer"] .btn-ghost`).
- **Submenus.** On `/chat` `finance-tasks-toggle`, `rail-market-specialists-toggle`, and `education-tasks-toggle` are `disabled`, `textContent` `tasks` / `specialists` / `tasks` (`tugas` / `spesialis` / `tugas` on `id`; `innerText` is uppercased by CSS), `aria-label` `Show tasks` / `Show specialists` / `Show tasks`, and `mode-finance` / `mode-market` / `mode-education` read their full names. Each chevron sits inside its mode's card: `finance-tasks-toggle.closest('[data-rail-row]')` is the same element as `mode-finance.closest('[data-rail-row]')`, and that wrapper is not the link (the testid stays on the link). Click `mode-finance`: the chevron enables with `aria-expanded="false"` and `rail-finance-tasks` has count 0 until you press the chevron. Press it: five rows. Click `mode-market`: the finance list closes (leaving the mode forgets the open), the market chevron enables still closed, and pressing it shows eleven rows. Pressing it again closes the block while you stay on `/market`. Click `mode-education`: the education chevron enables still closed, and pressing it shows four rows (`education-task-lesson`, `education-task-quiz`, `education-task-page`, `education-task-show`).
- **Hidden modes.** On a Legal desk, `mode-images` count 0; navigating to `/images` lands on `/chat`. Assert the **rail** testid, never `images-studio` — the keep-alive leaves a hidden studio pane mounted (see Gotchas).
- **First frames** (`rail-first-paint`). Route `GET /api/v1/workspaces` in the browser only (Playwright `context.route`, answer a five-mode desk after 800 ms, answer every non-GET yourself), install a `requestAnimationFrame` sampler before the page loads, and count `mode-<id>` links from the first frame that has the `<aside data-rail>`: with empty `localStorage`, the frames before the answer show `mode-chat` and four `rail-modes-skeleton-row`. The skeleton hands over to the five modes without moving `settings-link` **only if nothing else in the rail changes at the same moment**, and the recent-thread rows (`thread-item`) arrive on their own request: driven 2026-10-01 on `:3000` with two saved threads, the top of `settings-link` read 440 in every frame before the answer and 496 after it at 1280 (two 28px thread rows), and 440 then 351 at 375 (the rail starts collapsed there). So compare the `nav` scroll height and the top of `settings-link` only **after the thread list has settled** (wait for `rail-thread-list` to hold its final `thread-item` count), or answer `GET /api/v1/threads` yourself with an empty list in the same `context.route` so the thread rows cannot move it (not re-driven that way). On a second load in the same profile the frames show the five modes at once; a fourteen-mode desk shows fourteen on both loads. A deep link to `/documents` from a remembered five-mode list, with the host now answering fourteen, must stay on `/documents`. Do this at 1280 and 375.
- **Account links.** Each of `mode-knowledge`, `channels-link`, `workspaces-link`, `usage-link`, `settings-link` navigates to its path from both chrome states.
- **Pinned Account group** (`rail-pinned`). For each window size 1280x720, 1280x800, 1366x768 and 1440x900 (`setViewportSize`, reload, wait for `settings-link`): `settings-link`'s `getBoundingClientRect()` has `top >= 0` and `bottom <= innerHeight` and does not overlap `rail-footer`'s rect. Then read `rail-account-group`'s `data-more` and the nav's (`[data-tour="rail-modes"]`) `scrollHeight` against `clientHeight`: at `scrollTop` 0 `data-more` is `"true"` exactly when `scrollHeight` exceeds `clientHeight` by more than 1px (and `getComputedStyle(group, "::before").opacity` is `1`), and scrolling the nav to its end turns it `"false"`. Row height: every `[data-rail-row]` is 28px (its tile 22px) at a window height of 940px or less and 32px (24px) at 1000px. Driven 2026-10-01 on :3000 (full desk, light): `settings-link` bottom 663, 743, 711 and 776, `rail-footer` top 671, 751, 719 and 851, `data-more` `"true"` at the first three and `"false"` at 1440x900, rows 28px at all four and 32px at 1440x1000.
- **Buddy.** On an expanded rail `desk-buddy-dock` has count 1 on every page after onboarding, with `data-view` `body`, `chip` or `none` (`none` over the empty Chat's hero, an empty desk's character or a page stage; `chip` when the rail's list has no room for a 56px body, which is most laptop-height windows; the measured sizes are in mascot.md). Press `rail-collapse`: `desk-buddy-dock`, `desk-buddy` and `desk-buddy-chip` all have count 0; press `rail-expand`: the dock is back. When the view is `chip`, `desk-buddy-chip` is a child of `rail-footer`. Sizes, poses and reactions are driven in [mascot.md](./mascot.md).

## Gotchas

- `rail-collapse` and `rail-expand` are the same button with a state-swapped testid. Waiting for the other one is how you know the click landed.
- `product-brand` does **not** exist on a collapsed rail; only `product-logo` does, and it lives inside the compact switcher.
- `rail-resize` and `rail-thread-list` do not exist on a collapsed rail. Count 0 is the expected state (`new-chat-link` does: a collapsed rail keeps it as an icon, `apps/web/components/rail-recent-threads.tsx:49-62`).
- `[data-rail-row]` marks the row's card: the wrapper `div` for the Finance, Market and Education rows (the testid is on the link inside it) and the link itself for every other row, so count rows with `[data-rail-row]`, not `a[data-rail-row]` (19 on a full desk: 14 modes and 5 account links; while the skeleton shows, its four `rail-modes-skeleton-row` rows match too).
- The Account group is sticky, so `settings-link` is on screen while the Make rows below the fold are clipped by the nav: scroll the nav (or `scrollIntoView`) before clicking a mode that is out of view, and read `rail-account-group`'s `data-more` to know whether rows are hidden.
- Collapsed group labels are a 1px divider, not text. Never identify a group by its string — and never by string at all on an `id` desk.
- On a fresh desk `mode-research` and the rest are not in the DOM for the first few hundred milliseconds: wait for the fifth mode link, not for `mode-chat`, before asserting the desk, and do not count `rail-modes-skeleton-row` as modes. The session rows under Chat have placeholders as well (`rail-recent-skeleton-row`, not sessions: only `thread-item` is one) until `GET /api/v1/threads` answers; how many is what the last load showed, kept in `localStorage` `agentforge-rail-recent` (`{"rows":n,"more":bool}`), so on a fresh browser profile with sessions `settings-link` still moves once on that first load (it moved 56 px at 1280 before 2026-10-02, on every load); reload and it holds. Sample `settings-link`'s `getBoundingClientRect().top` from the first frame to prove it. Chat's `chat-key-status` also mounts once now: the panes no longer remount when the first desk id arrives, so the `GET /api/v1/chat` count on a cold load should be 1, not 2 (derived from the code and held by `lib/desk-pane-key.test.ts`; not yet measured in a browser).
- First paint is always an expanded 232px rail: collapse and width are read in mount effects, not in the state initializer. Wait for a named testid, not a fixed sleep, before screenshotting chrome.
- A hidden mode's studio is still in the DOM — SKILL.md **Harness-wide gotchas** G3. Driven on 2026-09-17: on a Legal desk `/images` redirected to `/chat` and `images-studio` still had count 1 with `isVisible()` false, so the rail testid is the only honest assertion for "this desk cannot reach that mode".
- Rail tabs come from the workspace, not from agents. A custom agent never adds a tab.
- The rail is `overflow-hidden`, so the switcher menu is portaled to `document.body`. Scope menu queries to the document.
- `mode-usage` and `mode-workspaces` do not exist. Usage and Workspaces are ACCOUNT links, not product modes.
