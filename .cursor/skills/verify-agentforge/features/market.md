# Market

Market is a job: a named desk picked by `?specialist=`, that desk's own watchlist of up to 15 tickers from any market, and a briefing instruction → the host fetches quotes, bars at that desk's depth, TradingView ratings, headlines, and macro levels on the user's machine, computes the rest (signals, rotation, sessions, swings, metals) in code, builds a chart per ticker, and the model writes a briefing (Bahasa Indonesia by default) over that data packet → guarded briefing → DOCX. It is analysis, not investment advice, and it never tells the reader to buy or sell now. Runs inside the host like Research, Data, and Finance: no server, no sidecar, no port, no Settings fields. Landed on `feat/market-mode` (2026-09-09, v2); see `docs/internal/0.14.25-changelog.md` and `docs/internal/market-mode-plan.md`. Map: [`docs/internal/maps/market-watch.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/market-watch.md).

Product: both · Instance: main :3000

## Sub-features

- `mode-header` (the shared `ModeHeader`, `data-mode="market"`, 0.15.0; there is no `market-header` testid) — title plus one outcome line in `expected-inputs`: "You get live prices and a chart per ticker, plus a written briefing — type up to 15 tickers, no file upload. Analysis, not investment advice." At a header 60rem wide or more the same `ModeHeader` also draws `buddy-stage-header` (Nultron in the art column, `apps/web/components/mode-header.tsx:66-73`). The rail's `rail-market-specialists-toggle` shows the word `specialists` (`Show specialists` stays in `aria-label` / `title`) and the list starts closed. Driven 2026-09-23: `rail-market-specialist-crypto` → `/market?specialist=crypto`, `market-specialist-current` `Crypto`; watchlist not edited, briefing not generated.
- `market-rail` (a recipe name, not a testid) is the way in: `agent-market`, then `agent-open-studio`, or `/market`. The chat-first rail has no Market submenu (`rail-market-mode`, `rail-market-specialists*` are count 0; they are `"modes"`-fallback only). The agent is chosen only by `?specialist=<id>` in the URL (see the named-agent bullet below); the chrome half belongs to [rail.md](./rail.md).
- `market-board` renders as soon as a chip exists, with no gateway key: one `market-board-card` per ticker carrying `market-board-price`, `market-board-close`, `market-board-facts`, `market-board-card-note`, and `market-board-chart`; `market-board-loading` is the skeleton grid while the fetch is in flight. `market-board-status` names the session, `market-board-refresh` refetches, `market-board-error` / `market-board-failures` explain a source that is down. Served by `POST /api/v1/market/board`, which fetches quotes, bars, and technicals only (no headlines, no macro, no model) and names no desk.
- Every price chart carries range buttons (1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y / 2Y, default 6M) and plots close + SMA50 + SMA200 on an axis that follows the data rather than anchoring at zero. The buttons live in `<chart testid>-ranges` (`market-board-chart-ranges` on the board, `market-chart-ranges` in the preview) and have **no testid of their own** — select them by `data-range`, whose values are lowercase (`1m`, `3m`, `6m`, `1y`, `2y`) even though the labels read `1M`…`2Y`. The plotted point count per range is 22 / 66 / 126 / 252 / 504. The **board** fetches 36 months, so even the 2Y window has the 199 bars of lead-in SMA200 needs; a shorter fetch silently drops both averages at 2Y. A **briefing** fetches its desk's own depth instead (6, 12 or 24 months), so a preview chart can legitimately be shorter than the board's for the same ticker.
- `market-studio` is the page. Inside it `market-inputs` is the form, and `market-watchlist-input` is the chip box (a `.input` with `.input-shell`: the rounded frame draws the focus, see **Focus ring** below) holding the text field `market-tickers`, the chips `market-ticker-chip`, the suggestion list `market-ticker-suggest` with `market-ticker-option` rows, and the refusal `market-ticker-notice`. The form always shows `market-starters` (one `market-specialist-starter` plus three `market-starter` buttons), including on a desk that already has chips. `market-studio-empty` ("Add a ticker above to see its price and chart.") appears only while the watchlist is empty — a state you reach by removing every chip, because a desk opens seeded from its own starter list. The specialist summary and the depth control are mounted above the starters while the watchlist is empty (`market-studio.tsx:388-393`) and at the end of the generate row once a chip exists, after `market-language` and `market-options-toggle` (`market-studio.tsx:478-479`), never both at once. The technical settings are behind `market-options-toggle`: the panel `market-options` holds `market-prompt` (with `market-prompt-reset`), `market-position`, `market-maxchars`, `market-studio-model`, `market-enhance`. `market-language`, `market-generate` and, while a run is live, `market-cancel` stay in the open. There is no `market-shell`.
- Market ships one pipeline and eleven named agents, and the agent is chosen by the **URL**, not in the studio and not in the rail: `/market?specialist=<id>` with `saham`, `forex`, `gold`, `crypto`, `commodities`, `indices`, `sector-rotation`, `scanner`, `summary`, `elliott-wave`, `news` in core order. **`market-specialist` - the old `<select>` - does not exist; its count is 0, and nothing in the UI links to a desk other than the default `saham`** (finding, 2026-10-12). The studio only reports the desk: `market-specialist-current` is the label, `market-specialist-hint` the one-line description, `market-specialist-sources` the provenance badge, and `market-specialist-starter` fills the watchlist with that agent's own starter tickers alongside the three `market-starter` presets. That header block is mounted above the starters while the watchlist is empty and at the end of the generate row, after the Options toggle, once a chip exists - never both at once. Choosing an agent (or a language) rewrites `market-prompt` to that agent's default instruction **only** while the box still holds one of ours; a prompt the user typed is left alone, and `market-prompt-reset` goes back to the current agent + language default. The chosen agent rides in the request body as `specialist`, comes back on the briefing, and is named in `market-briefing-specialist` above the artifact actions. The rewrite route needs no field of its own: it reads the agent off the briefing.
- Each agent keeps **its own watchlist**, per desk. The chips live in `localStorage` under `agentforge-market-watchlist:<workspaceId>:<specialist>` (a null workspace is the literal `home`), seeded from that agent's starter list the first time it is opened, deduped and capped at 15 on every write. Switching agents (by URL) swaps the chips and the board with them: the crypto desk's coins never appear on the gold desk, and a chip added to one desk is still there when you come back to it. Clearing the key is what makes a desk open from its starters again.
- `market-specialist-sources` sits under `market-specialist-hint` inside the same header block, so it rides along to whichever of the two placements is mounted. It is one muted line naming the providers behind that agent's harness, deduped and in source order: `Sumber: Yahoo Finance · TradingView · CoinGecko · Dihitung di perangkat` on an `id` desk, `Sources: …` with `Computed on device` on `en`. Quotes, history, headlines, macro, fundamentals, insiders and global news are Yahoo Finance; technicals are TradingView; the crypto section is CoinGecko; the sentiment section is the **two** venues `StockTwits · Reddit`; swings, metals, signals, rotation and sessions never leave the machine. So `crypto` reads `Yahoo Finance · TradingView · CoinGecko · StockTwits · Reddit` and `saham` ends `· StockTwits · Reddit` too, `elliott-wave` reads `Yahoo Finance · Dihitung di perangkat` (`Yahoo Finance · Computed on device` on `en`), and `scanner` reads all three of Yahoo, TradingView and the on-device label. The vendor names stay in English in both locales. A web-research desk earns no `· Web` item today: nothing the studio already loads says whether a web key is configured, and the badge does not promise a source it cannot see.
- **Depth.** `market-depth` is a `role="group"` segmented control mounted at the end of the generate row once a chip exists (after `market-language` and `market-options-toggle`), and beside the header block above the starters while the watchlist is empty — the same two placements as `market-specialist-current`, never both at once. It is headed `Analysis` / `Analisis` and holds `market-depth-quick` (labelled `Single analyst` / `Analis tunggal`, default, `aria-pressed="true"`) and `market-depth-team` (`Analyst team` / `Tim analis`); the **wire values stay `quick` and `team`** whatever the labels read, and the chosen one rides in the request body as `depth`. The track hugs its two buttons — it is `w-fit`, not stretched to the flex row. A desk whose harness names no analysts (`scanner`, `sector-rotation`, `elliott-wave`) keeps the control visible but `market-depth-team` `disabled`, with the muted line `market-depth-unavailable` saying why ("This desk has no analyst team, so a single analyst writes its briefing.") — on every other desk that line is `market-depth-hint` instead. Choosing `Analyst team` and then opening one of those three desks resets the state to quick, and coming back does **not** re-arm it.
- `market-team` is the analyst-team panel under the briefing sections and above `market-watchlist`, rendered **only** when the run carried a team (a quick briefing has none, so its count is 0). It starts collapsed: `market-team-toggle` (`aria-expanded`) opens four analyst cards `market-team-analyst-technical` / `-fundamentals` / `-sentiment` / `-news` (each with a confidence chip and `data-confidence` low / medium / high), the two debate columns `market-team-bull` and `market-team-bear`, and the three lenses `market-team-risk-aggressive` / `-neutral` / `-conservative`. Every string in it is model output rendered as plain text — no markdown, no HTML — so a note containing markup shows as characters.
- "Write a briefing" without a gateway key shows `market-error` "Quotes and charts work without a key. To write the briefing, add your API key in Settings." plus the `market-error-settings` link. The studio posts `/api/v1/market/stream`, so that refusal arrives as **HTTP 200 carrying an `event: job.error` frame** with `code: "runtime_stub"` and `status: 503` inside it — assert the `market-error` testid, never the HTTP status (SKILL.md **Harness-wide gotchas** G2). Only the non-stream `POST /api/v1/market` answers a real 503. Prices and charts keep working. A malformed body is 400 `invalid_request` whatever the runtime. An unresolvable symbol is listed in `market-failures`, never a crash. That refusal holds only on a keyless or stub desk: on a keyed desk (doctor `runtime: "ai"`, the operator's `:3000`) `market-generate` is a PAID live briefing (quotes, headlines, then one model call, or the analyst-team calls on `Analyst team`).
- `market-needs-key` (hosted only) is one muted line above `market-inputs` (`market-studio.tsx:377-382`, with the link `market-needs-key-settings`) saying quotes and charts still work and the briefing needs a key. While it shows, `market-generate` is `disabled` (`:447`). The gate is `useHostedNeedsKey` (`:116`), false off the hosted build, so it is count 0 on Personal and on webdev. `market-error` is drawn in one of two places, never both: above the form while the watchlist is empty (`:365-374`), and inside the generate card once a chip exists (`:432-442`).
- Live generate runs `market-progress` phases resolving → quotes → technicals → charts → news → macro → drafting (with "Still drafting… Ns" heartbeats) → verifying → saving. A desk that does not list headlines or macro simply skips those phases. A quiet source (a failed quote, headline, or macro read — not an unknown symbol) inserts `retrying` once and gathers the packet again; the run keeps the attempt with fewer holes. A draft figure that is not in the packet inserts `repairing` once, with no tools, and shows `market-progress-round` ("Round 1 of 1" plus the same label; the Market harness spec's `repairRounds` is 1), then verifying runs again; a figure that is still invented stays `[unverified figure]`. Those two labels follow the briefing language (`Trying that reading once more` / `Mencoba pembacaan itu sekali lagi`, `Rewriting a figure the packet does not have` / `Menulis ulang angka yang tidak ada di data`). A **team** run inserts analysts → debate → risk → synthesis in place of drafting; those four labels come from the web catalog (`market.progress.*`, so they read `Catatan analis`, `Ronde bullish dan bearish`, `Pembacaan risiko`, `Menulis briefing` on `id`) while every packet phase above still shows the host's English label. Stub generate still refuses before the packet, so retry and repair are proven by the host unit test, not by the keyless click.
- `market-preview` renders `market-clock` (session badge `data-session` pre / regular / post / closed + note), the model sections `market-section` with `market-section-regen`, `market-guard` (`data-clean`), `market-watchlist` rows, a `market-ticker-card` per ticker with `market-chart` (SVG line: close, SMA50, SMA200), `market-ticker-facts`, `market-headline` links, `market-macro` rows, `market-sources`, `market-disclaimer` (stamped by code, never editable), `market-failures` (sources that were down; a note, not a fail). Also in this view and not named above: `market-section-guarded` (the advice guard replaced part of that section), `market-headlines` and `market-ticker-failures` on each `market-ticker-card`, the `market-watchlist-row` / `market-macro-row` rows inside `market-watchlist` / `market-macro`, the shared artifact set `market-actions` with `market-actions-note`, and the rewrite panel `market-regen-panel` with `market-regen-prompt`, `market-regen-model`, `market-regen-file`, `market-regen-attach`, `market-regen-submit` — the regen ids come from `JobRegenPanel` mounted with `testIdPrefix="market"`, not from `market-briefing-view.tsx` itself, so a literal grep of that file misses them.
- `market-download` builds a DOCX with the disclaimer under the title and as the last paragraph; charts export as tables. `market-send-kb` (type Brief), `market-make-document`, `market-make-presentation` via the shared artifact actions.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose the Market agent on the left rail (`agent-market`), then `agent-open-studio`. Home has the tile. Pick the desk with `?specialist=<id>` before typing tickers.
- Open `http://127.0.0.1:3000/market` when the tab is unlocked. `?specialist=<id>` opens straight onto one desk.
- Legal / Marketing / Students presets do not add this tab unless the owner checks it.
- Nothing to install and nothing to configure beyond the gateway key. Web search is used when a Tavily/Brave key exists and the chosen desk asks for it; otherwise headlines come from Yahoo.

## Gotchas

- A briefing that reads "beli sekarang", "jual sekarang", "buy now", "sell now", "you should buy" is a C1 leak: the sentence is replaced with `[removed: directive language]` and counted in `market-guard`; the API rejects such a briefing on `/market/docx` and `/market/regenerate` with 502 `advice_leak`. Ranking, sentiment, targets, and levels are allowed.
- `[unverified figure]` in a section means the model wrote a number that is not in the packet, the headlines, or the position context, and the one rewrite still did. Index names (`S&P 500`, `Nasdaq 100`), `50-day` / `200-hari` labels, and `24/7` publisher clocks are not figures. One or two real flags on a long briefing is a note; many is a fail (check the packet block rounding). An unknown symbol is not retried. A source that is still quiet after the one extra gather is a note in `market-failures`, and the briefing still builds. The disclaimer is still stamped.
- **A fresh desk is not empty.** Every agent opens seeded from its own starter list, so `market-studio-empty` is count 0 on first load. `market-starters` is count 1 on that same open: the preset chips are not gated on an empty watchlist. Remove every chip to reach the empty message deliberately.
- Nothing about the desk is stored in the rail: there is no `agentforge-rail-market-specialists` key and no rail-prefs entry - do not assert one.
- **No rail rows.** The chat-first rail has no specialist rows to scroll. The `"modes"` fallback's height-adaptive list and `data-overflowing` hook are not driven here.
- A run outside 04:00–09:30 ET is labelled by `market-clock`; the default prompt tells the model to say so. That is correct behaviour, not a bug.
- Yahoo, TradingView or CoinGecko being down is a note when the briefing still builds from the rest; a fail only when the studio hangs or invents figures.
- The 15-ticker cap is silent. At `WATCHLIST_MAX = 15` (`packages/core/src/market/watch-schemas.ts:30`) the `market-tickers` input goes `disabled` with the placeholder "That is the maximum" / "Itu sudah batasnya"; `market-ticker-notice` is not shown, even though `watchlist.overflow` copy exists in both catalogs. Assert the `disabled` attribute, not a notice. Pasting past the cap adds chips up to 15 and stops, also silently.
- `market-download` and `market-position` are each on two elements once a result exists (the studio button and the shared artifact bar; the options input and the preview's position section). Use `.first()` or scope the query.
- Do not POST `/api/v1/market` as a substitute for the studio on a live proof — it is the only Market route that answers a real 503, so it proves the wrong thing about the studio.
