# Nultron 0.16.0

DPSBuddy is now called **Nultron**. This is the first version under the new name.

## The new name

- The app, the installer and the window are called Nultron. The app has a new logo, and Nultron, the character, now lives on the desk.
- **Nultron starts on a fresh desk.** Your DPSBuddy chats, workspaces and Knowledge Base do not move over, and you paste your Toko Token key again.
- The old DPSBuddy data stays on your computer. Nultron does not read it. Uninstalling Nultron removes it too.

## First run

- A new desk starts with Chat, Research, Images, Videos and Presentation. Add Documents, Finance and the other tools from **Workspaces**.
- A short tour shows the rail, Workspaces, the Chat box, the Knowledge Base and Settings. You can skip it at any step. Settings has **Replay the guide**.

## Chat

- **Open a folder on your computer.** Chat can read the files in that folder and suggest changes. Nothing is written until you approve each change. You see the changes before you approve.
- **Run code.** Chat and Data can run short Python or JavaScript programs inside the app, to calculate or check data. Each run has a 30-second limit.
- **Files on your desk.** Chat can create and edit notes and documents on your desk. Every change is kept as a version, and you can restore an older one from **Files** in the Knowledge Base. Documents and Presentation can use up to five of these files as sources.
- Chat can search your Knowledge Base while it writes a reply.
- Chat drops a source number that does not point to a real source.
- When you attach a picture or a clip, Chat looks at it. It does not make a new picture on that turn.
- The box, the header and the cards fit a narrow window. Extra details go into a menu.
- An empty chat shows a sky and a hello from Nultron.

## Models

- The default model and the **Recommended** list follow the gateway's newer models.
- If a model refuses a Thinking level, Nultron tries again once at a lower level and remembers that limit for a while.
- **Cancel** now also stops the model call that is running.

## Checked answers in every studio

Each studio now checks its own answer and fixes it once before you see it. None of this adds a new setting.

- **Research.** Each finding cites a page that was read and quotes it word for word. A new **Comparison** section shows where the sources agree, disagree, or where only one source says it.
- **Documents.** With a source attached, sentences and numbers the source does not support are removed.
- **Presentation.** The number of slides stays inside the length you chose. Each slide gets a layout, and empty speaker notes are filled.
- **Education.** A lesson is checked for an aim, an example, a self-check and a close. Quiz answers must come from your source.
- **Finance.** A sentence that describes a figure in the wrong direction is rewritten. Figures are still calculated in the app.
- **Data.** Nultron runs the breakdowns your columns allow. A sentence that states a number the query did not return is rewritten.
- **Market.** A source that did not answer is asked once more. An invented figure is rewritten.
- **Legal.** One readable file becomes the other side's draft. With no playbook, Nultron uses a built-in position for a credit agreement, an NDA or a general commercial contract. Quotes must be word for word.
- **Meeting.** A date or a figure that nobody said in the recording is removed or marked.
- **Images.** A short sentence becomes a full brief. "Poster" means portrait and "banner" means landscape, unless you choose a shape.
- **Videos.** A short sentence becomes a shot with a subject, a motion and a camera. A render that fails on the way is tried once more.
- **Music.** The lyric is checked before it is sung. A job that comes back with no songs is sent once more.
- **Edit.** Five plain requests in the box: "lift the dead air", "cut where the picture changes", "put the words on the picture", "make a clip of …", and "export this" (1080p).
- **Knowledge.** A question your notes do not cover now says so.

## The desk

- Nultron sits in the rail and reacts to what the app is doing. You can make it smaller.
- Mode headers no longer cover their text and buttons. Colours are easier to read in light and dark mode.
- The window opens faster. It no longer waits for the video tool check.
- In Presentation and Education, the shape toolbar keeps your shape selected.
- In Edit, the Play button and the timeline labels follow your language.

## Files to download

- **Windows.** `Nultron-Setup-0.16.0.exe`
- **macOS (preview).** `Nultron-0.16.0-mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple silicon (M1 and later), `Nultron-0.16.0-mac-x64.dmg` for Intel

## Windows

- Run `Nultron-Setup-0.16.0.exe` and follow the installer.
- The installer is not code-signed yet. If SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- If DPSBuddy is open, the installer closes it first.
- Nultron keeps its settings, workspaces and Knowledge Base in `%APPDATA%\Nultron`.

## macOS (preview)

- Open the `.dmg` and drag **Nultron** to Applications.
- This preview is not signed by Apple, so macOS will say it cannot verify the developer.
  - macOS 15 and later. Double-click the app, dismiss the warning, then open **System Settings**, **Privacy & Security**, and choose **Open Anyway**.
  - macOS 14 and earlier. Right-click **Nultron.app**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
  - If it still refuses, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Nultron.app` in Terminal.
- **Keychain.** When macOS asks, choose **Always Allow**.
- **Closing and quitting.** Closing the window keeps Nultron in the Dock. Click the Dock icon to come back. Cmd+Q quits.
- **Video editing needs ffmpeg** (`brew install ffmpeg`). Everything else works without it.
- **Updates on macOS are manual.** Download the new `.dmg` from here and drag it over the old app. Your settings and workspaces stay in `~/Library/Application Support/Nultron`.
- Please report anything odd on macOS on the issues page, with your macOS version and chip.
