# Nultron Enterprise ent-2026.10.02

The first pilot release of Nultron Enterprise, for a small group of invited testers.

## Signing in

- **Sign-in is by invitation.** Your organisation invites your work email. The first time, you enter your email and the invite code from your inbox, then a one-time code to confirm the address. After that, your email and a one-time code are enough.
- **The sign-in screen tells you where you stand.** It says plainly whether you are invited, already active, locked out for a while, or not invited, instead of a generic error.
- **Roles come from your organisation.** The owner and admins are set when people are invited.

## Keys

- **You can add the gateway key later.** A new desk opens without a key. Every screen that needs a model says so and links to Settings.
- **Only an owner or an admin can change the organisation's key.** Members use it but cannot replace or remove it.

## Your organisation's account

- **Erase account** permanently deletes the organisation's desks, files and people on this server and signs everyone out. Only the owner can do it. It takes two steps and the word DELETE.

## Language

- A new person's first sign-in uses the browser's language, English or Bahasa Indonesia. It can be changed in Settings.

## Known limits of this pilot

- Video editing exports need ffmpeg on the server, which this release does not include.
- Payments are not connected. Seats and plans are set by the operator.
